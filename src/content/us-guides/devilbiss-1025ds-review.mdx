## Performance caveats in the manual

The manual says flow and purity may be affected above 5,000 ft, above 95°F or above 93% relative humidity. If you live at altitude, read [oxygen at altitude](/us/guides/oxygen-at-altitude-denver/) and ask your supplier.

## Running cost

At 639 W for 24 hours, that's about 15.3 kWh a day, or roughly 466 kWh in a 30.4-day month. See our [electricity cost guide](/us/guides/oxygen-concentrator-electricity-cost/).

## Maintenance

Clean the filter door vents every 7 days, per the manual.

## Verdict

From the documents, the 1025DS is a lighter 10 LPM concentrator with a built-in oxygen alarm and bottle-filling support. Its purity drops toward the low end at high flow, and the manual flags reduced performance above 5,000 ft, so high-altitude users may prefer the Intensity 10.
