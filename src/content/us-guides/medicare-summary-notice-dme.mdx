## What's on each page (DME MSN)

| Page | What it shows | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Dashboard | Your details, deductible status, suppliers, approved and denied claims, total you may be billed | Name and last 4 digits of your Medicare number; supplier list |
| 2. Tips | How to review the notice, report fraud and get help | Fraud reporting if items you didn't get appear |
| 3. Claims | Assigned or unassigned claim, item descriptions, Approved column, Max You May Be Billed, notes | Each CPAP or oxygen item and date |
| 4. Denied claims | How to appeal; 120-day window on the sample | The appeal deadline |

## Checks for CPAP and oxygen users

- **Rental months:** each month's rental should appear once. For oxygen, Medicare pays only one supplier per rental month ([switching oxygen suppliers](/us/guides/switch-medicare-oxygen-supplier/)).
- **Supplies:** compare items and quantities against what arrived ([CPAP supplies schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/)).
- **Unassigned claims:** if a supplier doesn't accept assignment, you may owe more ([DME assignment explained](/us/guides/medicare-dme-assignment-explained/)).
- **Other insurance:** Medicare.gov suggests checking whether your other coverage, such as Medigap, pays what Medicare didn't ([Medigap and DME](/us/guides/medigap-cpap-oxygen-costs/)).

## If something is wrong

Call the supplier first; it may resubmit. If you still disagree, [appeal the decision](/us/guides/medicare-dme-appeal/).

## Verdict

Keep your supplier's bills and match them to every MSN. It's the quickest way to catch billing errors, duplicate rentals and denied resupply orders.
