Sources: Ready.gov, Florida and Texas emergency management, NOLA Ready, NOAA."
faqs:
  - q: "When is hurricane season?"
    a: "Ready.gov lists the Atlantic hurricane season as June 1 to November 30; NOLA Ready gives the same dates for New Orleans."
  - q: "Should I register with a special needs registry?"
    a: "Yes, if one covers you. Florida (Miami, Tampa) has the Florida Special Needs Registry, Texas (Houston) has STEAR, and New Orleans asks residents to list medical needs in a Smart911 profile. None of them guarantees help or a shelter place, per the agencies."
  - q: "What should I pack for CPAP or oxygen if I evacuate?"
    a: "Ready.gov advises including medication in your go bag, since you may not have access to supplies for days or even weeks after a hurricane. Add your device, power supply, batteries, mask, tubing and your supplier's contact details."
  - q: "Is it safe to plug in my concentrator after flooding?"
    a: "Ready.gov says not to touch electrical equipment if it's wet or if you're standing in water. Have your supplier check flood-exposed equipment."
  - q: "Will Medicare replace equipment lost in a hurricane?"
    a: "Medicare.gov says that in most cases Original Medicare covers repair or replacement of DME it already paid for that was lost or damaged in a disaster, from a Medicare-approved supplier. Medicare Advantage members should call their plan."

## After the storm

- Don't touch electrical equipment if it's wet or you're standing in water (Ready.gov).
- People with lung conditions shouldn't enter buildings with water leaks or visible or smelled mold (Ready.gov).
- Run generators outdoors only ([power outage plan](/us/guides/power-outage-plan-cpap-oxygen/)).
- Lost or damaged equipment: see [replacing CPAP or oxygen after a disaster](/us/guides/replace-cpap-oxygen-after-disaster/).

## Verdict

For CPAP and oxygen users on the Gulf and Florida coasts, the main risk is losing power or having to evacuate, not the heat itself. Register with your local program, pack early, and agree a backup plan with your supplier before June.
