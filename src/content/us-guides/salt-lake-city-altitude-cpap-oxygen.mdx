## Cold-weather limits

Most manuals we've read list a minimum operating temperature of 41°F (50°F for the Oxlife Independence). January and December normal highs are under 40°F, so:

- keep CPAP and home concentrators in heated rooms;
- don't run a device that's been left in a cold car until it has warmed into its operating range;
- in cold bedrooms, expect more rainout; see [CPAP rainout](/us/guides/cpap-rainout/) and [heated tubing](/us/guides/climatelineair-heated-tubing/).

## Mountain trips

Mountain destinations can be higher than the city. Check each device's rated altitude against your destination before you go, and ask your clinician whether you need a different oxygen setting there. The research on oxygen at altitude is covered in our [Denver altitude guide](/us/guides/oxygen-at-altitude-denver/).

## Verdict

Salt Lake City's elevation is within every CPAP and POC rating we checked; winter cold is the bigger practical issue. If you use a home concentrator and live above 5,000 ft, ask your supplier about performance.
