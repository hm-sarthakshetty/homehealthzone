## Magnet warning

ResMed says masks with magnetic parts are contraindicated where the user, or anyone in close contact during use, has active implants that interact with magnets (such as pacemakers or implantable defibrillators) or metallic implants such as aneurysm clips, embolic coils, stents, some hearing implants, ocular implants or metal splinters in the eye. Ask your doctor if any apply. Nasal options without magnets include the [AirFit N30 and P10](/us/guides/airfit-n20-vs-n30/).

## Verdict

From the documents, the F40 ties with the AirTouch F30i as ResMed's quietest full-face option on paper when fitted with the QuietAir vent ring, with a minimal under-nose design and a mid-range price. Choose the F20 instead if you need pressures above 30 cmH2O, or the [F30i](/us/guides/airfit-f30i-vs-x30i-vs-airtouch-f30i/) if you want the tube on top of your head.
