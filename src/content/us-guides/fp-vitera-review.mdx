## Who it suits

People who breathe through the mouth and prefer a seal over the nose bridge, or who need a larger cushion.

## Alternatives

The [Evora Full](/us/guides/fp-evora-full-review/) is the smaller under-nose option; ResMed's [AirFit F20 and F30i](/us/guides/airfit-f30i-vs-x30i-vs-airtouch-f30i/) are the main rivals.

## Verdict

The F&P Vitera is a traditional full-face mask covering the nose and mouth, rated 4–30 cmH2O with 29.8 dBA sound power per F&P. It comes in Small, Medium and Large. It cost $149 complete at CPAP.com on 13 October 2026 ($114 without headgear), with $54 replacement cushions.
