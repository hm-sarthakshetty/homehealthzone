## What each program pays (2026)

| Program | Pays | Monthly income limit (individual / couple) | Resource limit (individual / couple) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| QMB | Part A premiums (if you pay one), Part B premiums, deductibles, coinsurance and copayments | $1,350 / $1,824 | $9,950 / $14,910 |
| SLMB | Part B premiums only | $1,616 / $2,184 | $9,950 / $14,910 |
| QI | Part B premiums only; reapply yearly | $1,816 / $2,455 | $9,950 / $14,910 |

Income limits are slightly higher in Alaska and Hawaii, and Medicare.gov says you may qualify in some states even above these limits; contact your state.

## What QMB means for CPAP and oxygen

Normally you pay the Part B deductible and 20% of the Medicare-approved amount for DME ([Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/durable-medical-equipment-dme-coverage)). With QMB, Medicare providers aren't allowed to bill you for those amounts. You may get a bill for a small Medicaid copayment, if one applies.

**Show at every order:** your Medicare card and your Medicaid or QMB card. A Medicare Summary Notice also shows QMB status ([how to read your MSN](/us/guides/medicare-summary-notice-dme/)).

## Verdict

If your income is near these limits, apply through your state: QMB can remove your CPAP and oxygen cost-sharing entirely. SLMB and QI help only with premiums.
