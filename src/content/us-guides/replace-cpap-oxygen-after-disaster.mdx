## Original Medicare vs Medicare Advantage

| Situation | Original Medicare | Medicare Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment Medicare paid for is lost or damaged | Repair or replacement covered in most cases | Contact your plan |
| Where to get it | Medicare-approved supplier only | Plan's rules |
| While equipment is repaired | Rentals usually covered (Medicare.gov's example: wheelchairs) | Contact your plan |
| Declared disaster area | Usual rules may change for a short time | Contact your plan |

## Steps after a storm

1. If equipment got wet, don't plug it in or touch it while you're standing in water ([Ready.gov hurricanes](https://www.ready.gov/hurricanes)).
2. Call your supplier: describe the damage and ask about a loaner.
3. Photograph the damage, as Ready.gov advises for property damage.
4. Ask your doctor for any new paperwork the supplier needs.
5. Watch your [Medicare Summary Notice](/us/guides/medicare-summary-notice-dme/) and [appeal](/us/guides/medicare-dme-appeal/) if a replacement claim is denied.

## Verdict

Plan ahead with your supplier, but if the worst happens, Original Medicare usually covers replacement of equipment it already paid for. Use a Medicare-approved supplier.
