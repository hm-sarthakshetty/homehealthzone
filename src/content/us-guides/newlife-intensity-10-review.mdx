## Purity: two numbers

CAIRE's manual specifies 90% (+5.5/−3%), while its product web page says 92% from 2 to 9 LPM. We can't reconcile these. Ask your supplier to check purity with an oxygen analyzer at your prescribed flow if it matters for your [oxygen targets](/us/guides/oxygen-saturation-targets-copd/).

## Running cost

At 600 W for 24 hours, that's 14.4 kWh a day, or roughly 438 kWh in a 30.4-day month. See the [electricity cost guide](/us/guides/oxygen-concentrator-electricity-cost/).

## Verdict

From the documents, the Intensity 10 is the heavy-duty pick: dual flow, high altitude rating and long tubing. It's heavier and larger than the [DeVilbiss 1025DS](/us/guides/devilbiss-1025ds-review/), which is the lighter 10 LPM option.
