## Who it suits

Nose breathers who want minimal contact and a low-noise mask, at pressures up to 25 cmH2O.

## Alternatives

ResMed's [AirFit N20 and N30](/us/guides/airfit-n20-vs-n30/) are nasal options; the [Evora Nasal](/us/guides/fp-evora-nasal-review/) is F&P's compact nasal mask.

## Verdict

The F&P Nova Micro is a nasal pillows mask rated 4–25 cmH2O with 21.4 dBA sound power and 13.45 dBA sound pressure per F&P, the quietest F&P mask in our guides on paper. It cost $99 complete at CPAP.com on 13 October 2026, with $29 replacement pillows.
