## Las Vegas temperatures (airport, 1991–2020)

| Month | Normal high | Normal low |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May | 88.5°F | 66.1°F |
| June | 99.4°F | 75.8°F |
| July | 104.5°F | 82.0°F |
| August | 102.8°F | 80.6°F |
| September | 94.9°F | 72.4°F |

Station elevation: 649.5 m (about 2,131 ft).

## What this means

| Situation | Guidance from the documents |
| --- | --- |
| CPAP at night | Normal summer lows (75.8–82.0°F) are inside the 95°F limit; use air conditioning |
| POC outdoors at midday | July's normal 104.5°F is just over the 104°F limit; seek shade and cool breaks |
| Device left in a car | Storage ranges go to 158°F (AirSense 11, Rove 6), operation doesn't; let it cool |

## Verdict

Las Vegas is a little milder than Phoenix but still exceeds CPAP operating limits in summer daytime. Keep equipment air-conditioned and plan outdoor oxygen use around the heat.
