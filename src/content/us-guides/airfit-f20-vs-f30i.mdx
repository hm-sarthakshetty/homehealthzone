## Magnet warning (both masks)

ResMed's guides say to keep the magnets at least 2 in away from active implants or medical devices that magnets can affect, and list contraindications including aneurysm clips and metal splinters in one or both eyes. The magnetic field is under 400 mT. If you or your bed partner has a medical implant, ask your doctor before using either mask. A magnet-free option on the nasal side is the [P10 or N30i](/us/guides/airfit-p10-vs-n30i/).

## Verdict

The F20 is the cheaper, higher-pressure traditional full-face mask. The F30i is the quieter, more minimal design with tubing that runs over your head. For mouth breathers who move a lot, the F30i is worth trying first; for high pressures, the F20.
