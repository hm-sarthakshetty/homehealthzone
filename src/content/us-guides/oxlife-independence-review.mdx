## Verdict

From the documents, the Independence fills a gap that lightweight pulse-only POCs don't: real continuous flow up to 3 LPM, 24/7 duty, a high altitude rating and a 5-year warranty. The costs are weight and short battery life at continuous settings. Plan around the manual's battery table, not the marketing numbers.
