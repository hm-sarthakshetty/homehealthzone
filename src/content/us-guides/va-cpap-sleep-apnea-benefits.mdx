If you're an enrolled Veteran, the VA can supply your CPAP and its parts at no charge. Here's how it works, from VA.gov and the federal rating schedule.

## Eligibility for free CPAP supplies

According to VA.gov, all of these must be true ([VA: order medical supplies](https://www.va.gov/health-care/order-medical-supplies/)):

| # | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Enrolled in VA health care |
| 2 | Registered as a patient at a VA medical center |
| 3 | Your VA provider prescribed the supplies or the CPAP machine |

If you meet these, CPAP supplies are free of charge.

## What you can order

VA.gov lists these CPAP supplies ([VA](https://www.va.gov/health-care/order-medical-supplies/)):

| Supply | Supply |
| --- | --- |
| Chinstrap | Mask with headgear |
| Hoses (tubing) | Power cord |
| Filters (disposable or non-disposable) | Replacement cushion |
| Mask liners | SD memory card |
| Water chamber | |

For how often parts wear out, see our [CPAP supplies replacement schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/).

## How to order

| Method | How | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online | Sign in on VA.gov (My HealtheVet) | Only if you've ordered these supplies from VA within the past 2 years |
| Phone | VA Denver Logistics Center (number on VA.gov; TTY 711), Mon–Fri 8:15 a.m.–7:30 p.m. ET; select 2 for CPAP | A representative takes your order |
| Mail | VA Form 2346b (included with your last order) to VA Denver Logistics Center, PO Box 25166, Denver, CO 80225-0166 | |

Supplies should arrive in 7 to 10 days, longer if out of stock. VA advises reordering 30 days before you need more ([VA](https://www.va.gov/health-care/order-medical-supplies/)).

## If your CPAP machine isn't working

VA.gov: "Contact your local VA health facility. Your VA provider can order a new machine for you" ([VA](https://www.va.gov/health-care/order-medical-supplies/)). If you have a recalled Philips device, also see [Philips CPAP recall](/us/guides/philips-cpap-recall-consent-decree/).

## Disability ratings for sleep apnea (38 CFR 4.97, DC 6847)

| Rating | Criteria (sleep apnea syndromes: obstructive, central, mixed) |
| --- | --- |
| 100% | Chronic respiratory failure with carbon dioxide retention or cor pulmonale, or requires tracheostomy |
| 50% | Requires use of a breathing assistance device such as a CPAP machine |
| 30% | Persistent daytime hypersomnolence |
| 0% | Asymptomatic but with documented sleep-disordered breathing |

Source: [38 CFR 4.97](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-4/subpart-B/subject-group-ECFRf2e4ca7a0e2d0d5/section-4.97), as published in the eCFR on 13 October 2026. The VA decides any rating on your claim. HHZ doesn't give claims advice; a VA-accredited representative can help.

## VA and other coverage

You can have VA care and Medicare or other insurance, but they're separate. VA CPAP supplies come through your VA prescription. If you buy outside the VA, compare [CPAP without insurance](/us/guides/cpap-without-insurance-cost/) and [Medicare CPAP coverage](/us/guides/medicare-cpap-coverage-compliance/).

## Verdict

If you're an enrolled Veteran with a VA CPAP prescription, use the VA's free supply program: order online (if you've ordered in the last 2 years), by phone or by mail, and reorder 30 days ahead. Contact your VA facility for machine problems. For compensation, sleep apnea is rated under DC 6847, and requiring CPAP corresponds to 50% under the current schedule.
