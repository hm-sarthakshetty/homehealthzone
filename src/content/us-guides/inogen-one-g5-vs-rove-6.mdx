## The G5 manual conflict

Two revisions of the G5 user manual, both on inogen.com, give different specifications:

| Spec | G5 manual Rev B | G5 manual Rev C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single battery | Up to 4.5 h | Up to 6.5 h |
| Double battery | Up to 9 h | Up to 13 h |
| Single battery recharge | Up to 4 h | Up to 3 h |
| Sound at setting 2 | 39 dBA | 38 dBA |

Inogen doesn't explain the change. If you're buying a used G5, the battery that comes with it and its age matter more than either figure; batteries are rated for 500 full cycles.

## Buying a used G5: checklist

- Ask for the run hours and when the sieve columns were last changed (rated 1 year).
- Ask how many cycles the battery has, or budget for a new one.
- Get a written warranty from the seller.
- Confirm with your prescriber that pulse dose works for you, including during sleep.
- Check that the provider can service it, since Inogen now sells the Rove 6.

See [used and refurbished concentrators](/us/guides/used-refurbished-oxygen-concentrator/).

## Verdict

The documents show the Rove 6 is essentially a G5 with a longer rated life (8 vs 5 years). Buy new if you can; it's the safer long-term purchase. A used G5 is reasonable only at a clear discount, with a fresh battery, recent columns and a seller warranty.
