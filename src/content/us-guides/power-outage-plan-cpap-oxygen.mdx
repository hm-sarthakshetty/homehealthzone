> **Sources:** [Ready.gov power outages](https://www.ready.gov/power-outages); [HHS emPOWER](https://empowermap.hhs.gov/); manufacturer documents in our linked guides. Checked 13 October 2026. This isn't medical advice; make your plan with your clinician and supplier.

## Before an outage

| Step | Why (source) |
| --- | --- |
| Talk to your clinician and supplier about an outage plan | Ready.gov advises it for electric medical devices |
| Plan batteries and alternatives | Ready.gov |
| Ask your oxygen supplier about backup | Suppliers handle equipment and contents; see [Medicare oxygen coverage](/us/guides/medicare-oxygen-coverage/) |
| Install carbon monoxide detectors | Ready.gov (generator safety) |
| Know a community location with power | Ready.gov, for extreme heat or cold |

## Who counts as electricity-dependent

HHS emPOWER's electricity-dependent DME list includes oxygen concentrators and BiPAP machines (among other devices). CPAP isn't on it, but if you rely on CPAP nightly, plan as if it were.

## Backup options

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| POC batteries | Runtimes vary by setting; see [Inogen Rove 6](/us/guides/inogen-rove-6-review/), [FreeStyle Comfort](/us/guides/caire-freestyle-comfort-review/) and [Independence](/us/guides/oxlife-independence-review/) reviews |
| CPAP batteries and power stations | Humidifier and heated tubing use more power; see [CPAP battery options](/us/guides/cpap-battery-camping-power/) |
| Generator | Outdoors only, at least 20 ft from windows (Ready.gov) |
| Leave for a community location | In extreme heat or cold (Ready.gov) |

## Heat and cold during an outage

Device operating ranges are typically 41–95°F for CPAP and 41–104°F for portable concentrators; see our [Phoenix heat](/us/guides/phoenix-heat-cpap-oxygen/) and [Salt Lake City](/us/guides/salt-lake-city-altitude-cpap-oxygen/) guides.

## Verdict

Write the plan down: who to call, how long your batteries last at your setting, where your backup oxygen is, and where you'll go if it's too hot or cold. Never run a generator indoors.
