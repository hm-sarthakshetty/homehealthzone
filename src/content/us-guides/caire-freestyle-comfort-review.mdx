## What autoSAT changes

Without autoSAT, the pulse per breath shrinks as you breathe faster, so oxygen per minute stays fixed. With autoSAT, CAIRE's manual shows the pulse held constant up to a cap:

| Breaths per minute | Setting 3, standard | Setting 3, autoSAT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 42.0 mL | 31.5 mL |
| 20 | 31.5 mL | 31.5 mL |
| 30 | 21.0 mL | 31.5 mL |
| 40 | 15.8 mL | 26.3 mL |

That matters on exertion, when breathing speeds up. Whether it's enabled is a provider setting, and CAIRE says autoSAT is "available in select geographies".

## Verdict

From the documents, the FreeStyle Comfort stands out for autoSAT and an oxygen monitor, with a 3-year warranty that includes the sieve beds. It tops out at setting 5 (1,050 mL/min), below the six-setting Rove 6 and OxyGo NEXT, and its manual battery times are lower than the marketing.
