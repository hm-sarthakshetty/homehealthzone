Many people with a portable oxygen concentrator (POC) wonder whether they can skip the big home unit at night. The honest answer: sometimes, if it's tested on you. Here's what the manufacturers say.

## The key issue: triggering during sleep

Pulse-dose POCs deliver oxygen only when they sense you inhale.

| What the manufacturer says | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Rove 6 is "designed to provide medical-grade oxygen delivery all day, every day" | [Inogen](https://www.inogen.com/product/rove6-system/) |
| Contraindicated "in persons whose breathing during normal resting is unable to trigger the device" | [Rove 6 manual](https://www.inogen.com/rove6-user-manuals/pdf/96-13595-00-01%20A%20Inogen%20Rove%206%20US%20User%20Manual_EN_ES_February%202026.pdf) |
| Settings must be "individually determined or prescribed for you at your specific activity levels" | [Rove 6 manual](https://www.inogen.com/rove6-user-manuals/pdf/96-13595-00-01%20A%20Inogen%20Rove%206%20US%20User%20Manual_EN_ES_February%202026.pdf) |
| Detects shallow breathing down to 0.12 cmH₂O and delivers oxygen within the first 250 ms of inhalation | [Rove 6 manual](https://www.inogen.com/rove6-user-manuals/pdf/96-13595-00-01%20A%20Inogen%20Rove%206%20US%20User%20Manual_EN_ES_February%202026.pdf) |
| "Some respiratory efforts of the patient might not trigger the conserving function" | [FreeStyle Comfort manual](https://files.caireinc.com/Manuals/MN232-0_B.pdf) |
| Pulse settings must be determined individually "at rest, during exercise, and when traveling" | [FreeStyle Comfort manual](https://files.caireinc.com/Manuals/MN232-0_B.pdf) |

Sleep is an activity level of its own. A setting that works on a walk may not be what you need asleep.

## Backup pulses if no breath is detected

| Model | No breath detected for | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inogen Rove 6 | 60 seconds | Alarm (if enabled) and pulses at 20 per minute |
| OxyGo NEXT | 60 seconds | Enters auto pulse mode until a breath is detected |
| CAIRE FreeStyle Comfort | 15 seconds | Doses at 20 breaths per minute until inhalation is detected |

Sources: the [Rove 6](https://www.inogen.com/rove6-user-manuals/pdf/96-13595-00-01%20A%20Inogen%20Rove%206%20US%20User%20Manual_EN_ES_February%202026.pdf), [OxyGo NEXT](https://virteomdevcdn.blob.core.windows.net/site-oxygo-com/uploaded_media/oxygo_com/Marketing_Resources/96-08857-00-01UserManualOxyGoNext-English-Spanish-French-REDUX__1741716006.pdf) and [FreeStyle Comfort](https://files.caireinc.com/Manuals/MN232-0_B.pdf) manuals. On the Rove 6, the audible no-breath alarm can be switched off, but backup pulsing still happens. If you can't hear alarms or communicate discomfort, Inogen says to consult your clinician before use.

## The evidence so far

Inogen sponsored a single-arm pilot study ([NCT06000332](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06000332)) of 20 adults using the Rove 6 overnight for at least 6 hours. It measured whether participants could keep SpO₂ at or above 90% for more than 30% of sleep time, and whether settings needed raising. ClinicalTrials.gov lists it as completed in December 2023. No results were shown on the registry page when we checked on 13 October 2026, so it can't yet tell you whether pulse dose works overnight.

## Practical rules for night use

| Rule | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get your night setting confirmed by your clinician | Settings are individual and activity-specific | Inogen, CAIRE |
| Don't use with CPAP | Rove 6 manual: not with CPAP, humidifiers or nebulizers; NEXT manual: not with humidifiers, nebulizers or other equipment | Inogen, OxyGo |
| Run on AC power at the bedside | Batteries rarely last a full night at higher settings | Manuals ([battery life](/us/guides/portable-oxygen-concentrator-battery-life/)) |
| Keep the cannula off bedding when the unit is on but not in use; turn it off when not in use | "Oxygen makes materials flammable" | Inogen |
| No open flames within 6.56 ft (2 m); no smoking | Enriched oxygen accelerates combustion | [Inogen spec sheet](https://www.inogen.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Inogen-Rove-6-Spec-Sheet.pdf) |
| Have backup oxygen for outages or failure | CAIRE advises backup (e.g. cylinder) oxygen | CAIRE |

## If you also use CPAP

Pulse POCs aren't the route for oxygen with CPAP. ResMed lists oxygen flow limits in liters per minute for its CPAPs (15 L/min on the AirSense 11). See [CPAP with oxygen](/us/guides/cpap-with-oxygen/).

## Verdict

A POC can be your night-time oxygen only if your clinician tests and prescribes it for sleep, on the same model and accessories. Pulse dose depends on your breath triggering it, and the backup pulses are a safety net, not a guarantee. Run it on wall power, keep it away from flames, and don't pair it with CPAP. If triggering is a concern, ask about continuous flow ([pulse vs continuous](/us/guides/pulse-dose-vs-continuous-flow/)).
