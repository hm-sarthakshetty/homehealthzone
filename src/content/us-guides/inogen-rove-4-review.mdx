## Source conflicts we found

- **Weight:** the page title says "2.8 lb", but the same page's specs and the manual say 2.9 lb with the standard battery.
- **Recharge time:** the product page lists up to 3 hours for the standard battery; the manual lists up to 3 hours 30 minutes for the BA-400.
- **Service life:** the manual states 8 years. Inogen's product FAQ says "up to 8 years when used less than 5 hours per day".

## Verdict

From the documents, the Rove 4 is the lightest Inogen unit, at 2.9 lb, and comes with Inogen's 8-year rated service life. The trade-off is a ceiling of 840 mL/min and short standard-battery runtime. If your prescription might rise, the [Rove 4 vs Rove 6](/us/guides/inogen-rove-4-vs-rove-6/) comparison is worth reading first.
