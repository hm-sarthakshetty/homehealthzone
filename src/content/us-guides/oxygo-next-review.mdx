## Safety and use notes from the manual

- Prescription only. Settings must be prescribed for you. The NEXT is not intended to be life sustaining.
- It isn't designed to be used with a humidifier or nebulizer, or connected with any other equipment.
- If no breath is detected for 60 seconds, it enters auto pulse mode until a breath is detected ([sleeping with a POC](/us/guides/portable-oxygen-concentrator-for-sleeping/)).
- Battery care: avoid long use below 41°F or above 95°F, store batteries at 40–50% charge, and don't leave them unused more than 90 days.
- For flying, carry batteries for at least 150% of the trip. Extra batteries must be individually protected and carried on ([FAA rules](/us/guides/faa-portable-oxygen-concentrator-rules/)).
- Sieve beds are user-replaceable, with a 1-year expected life. Use only OxyGo-specified columns.

## Verdict

If your clinician has prescribed a pulse setting of 6 or below and you've been tested on pulse dose, the OxyGo NEXT is a strong value. It matches the Rove 6's output and size for less, with a longer warranty. Choose the Rove 6 instead if you value its longer rated service life. Either way, buy the double battery if you're out all day.
