## Who it suits

Nose breathers on lower pressures who want to try both a nasal cushion and pillows without buying a second frame.

## Alternatives

If you need more than 20 cmH2O, the [Evora Nasal](/us/guides/fp-evora-nasal-review/) and [Nova Micro](/us/guides/fp-nova-micro-review/) are rated to 25 cmH2O. See [Solo vs Evora Nasal](/us/guides/fp-solo-vs-evora-nasal/).

## Verdict

The F&P Solo is one frame that takes either a nasal cushion or a nasal pillows cushion. F&P rates it 4–20 cmH2O, the lowest maximum of the F&P masks in our guides, with 31.5 dBA sound power. Nasal and pillows versions were each $119 complete at CPAP.com on 14 October 2026; a nasal cushion was $39 and pillows $34.
