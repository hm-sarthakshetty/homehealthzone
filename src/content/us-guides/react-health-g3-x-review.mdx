## Strengths on paper

- Lower price than the AirSense 11 at the retailers we checked.
- A 5-year device warranty, though it starts at sale to the dealer.
- Two data paths (SD card and cellular), useful for [Medicare compliance](/us/guides/medicare-cpap-coverage-compliance/).
- An alternative brand for people replacing a recalled Philips machine ([DreamStation alternatives](/us/guides/philips-dreamstation-cpap-alternatives/)).

## Limitations

| Limitation | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Altitude | 2,300 m (about 7,500 ft) maximum; check this if you live or travel higher |
| Warranty start | Counted from React Health's sale to the dealer, so a unit that sat in stock has less left |
| Comfort settings | Reslex and Smart features depend on clinician setup |
| Data app | We found no patient app comparable to myAir in the documents we read |

## Verdict

On paper, the G3 X is a capable, lower-cost full-size auto CPAP with a heated humidifier, cellular data and a 5-year warranty. Its main limit is the 2,300 m altitude ceiling. If you live below that and want to save versus the AirSense 11, it's worth asking your supplier about.
