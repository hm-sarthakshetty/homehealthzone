## Who it suits

Nose breathers who find pillows uncomfortable in the nostrils but want something smaller than a full nasal mask.

## Alternatives

The [Nova Micro](/us/guides/fp-nova-micro-review/) is F&P's pillows option; ResMed's [AirFit N20 and N30](/us/guides/airfit-n20-vs-n30/) and [AirTouch N20](/us/guides/airtouch-n20-vs-airfit-n20/) are rivals.

## Verdict

The F&P Evora Nasal is a compact nasal mask that sits under the nose, rated 4–25 cmH2O with 26.8 dBA sound power per F&P. The fit pack includes Small, Medium and Large seals, with a Wide seal sold separately. It cost $95 at CPAP.com on 13 October 2026, with $31 replacement cushions.
