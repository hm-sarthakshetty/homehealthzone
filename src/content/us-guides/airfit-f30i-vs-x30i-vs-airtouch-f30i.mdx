## Which to choose

| You... | Better fit |
| --- | --- |
| Want the lowest price and easy washing | AirFit F30i |
| Prefer fabric against your skin | AirTouch F30i |
| Want the quietest published figures | AirTouch F30i with QuietAir |
| Like nasal pillows but breathe through your mouth | AirFit X30i |
| Have a magnet-sensitive implant | None of these; ask your doctor |

## Verdict

Because the cushions are interchangeable on one frame, you can switch styles without starting over. The silicone AirFit F30i is the value pick; the AirTouch F30i trades cost for a fabric cushion and lower published sound; the X30i suits pillows users who need mouth coverage. For a traditional full-face seal or pressures above 30 cmH2O, see [AirFit F20 vs F30i](/us/guides/airfit-f20-vs-f30i/).
