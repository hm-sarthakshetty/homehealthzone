If you use CPAP, home oxygen or other durable medical equipment (DME), how you get Medicare matters day to day: which supplier you can call, what you pay, and whether you need approval first. Here's the comparison from Medicare.gov and CMS.

## What Original Medicare looks like for CPAP and oxygen

- **CPAP:** coverage follows the national and DME MAC policies: in-person evaluation, qualifying sleep test, 12-week adherence trial ([Medicare CPAP coverage](/us/guides/medicare-cpap-coverage-compliance/)).
- **Oxygen:** 36-month rental with ongoing supplier obligations up to 5 years ([Medicare home oxygen coverage](/us/guides/medicare-oxygen-coverage/), [switching oxygen suppliers](/us/guides/switch-medicare-oxygen-supplier/)).
- **Assignment matters:** a participating supplier must accept assignment and can charge you only the coinsurance and deductible on the approved amount ([Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/durable-medical-equipment-dme-coverage)).

## What to check in a Medicare Advantage plan

| Question | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Is my current CPAP or oxygen supplier in the plan's approved list? | Plan provider/supplier directory; call the plan |
| What's the DME cost-sharing (coinsurance or copay)? | Evidence of Coverage / Summary of Benefits |
| Is prior authorization needed for CPAP, oxygen or resupply? | Evidence of Coverage; call the plan |
| How are replacement masks and supplies handled? | Plan rules; compare with the [replacement schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/) |
| What's the yearly out-of-pocket maximum? | Summary of Benefits |
| How does the plan handle travel? | Plan's rules on out-of-network and out-of-area care |

## Who tends to do better where

These are trade-offs, not rules. Check real plan documents.

- **Original Medicare (plus Medigap)** suits people who want the widest supplier choice, travel within the U.S., or use expensive DME long term and want the 20% covered by Medigap.
- **Medicare Advantage** can suit people whose preferred suppliers are in-network and who value the yearly out-of-pocket cap and extra benefits. Confirm DME cost-sharing and prior authorization rules first.

## Verdict

For DME, the biggest practical differences are supplier choice and prior authorization. If you depend on CPAP or oxygen, look at each Medicare Advantage plan's approved supplier list and DME cost-sharing before enrolling, and compare that with Original Medicare's 20% coinsurance plus any Medigap premium.
