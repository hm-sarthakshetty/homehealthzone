## How to choose

| Situation | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| Setting 1–3, short outings, weight is key | Rove 4 |
| Setting 4 at rest but higher on exertion | Rove 6 |
| Setting 5 or 6 | Rove 6 (the Rove 4 can't deliver it) |
| Long flights or full days out | Rove 6 with extended battery |
| COPD that may progress | Rove 6 for headroom |

## Verdict

The documents show the same core technology in two sizes. The Rove 6 offers more output and roughly double the battery life for less money on Inogen's own price list, so for most people it's the better value. The Rove 4 earns its place only if carrying 1.9 lb less makes a real difference and your prescription is firmly at setting 4 or below.
