## Who it suits

Mouth breathers who want a smaller full-face mask that doesn't sit on the nose bridge.

## Alternatives

The [ResMed AirFit F40](/us/guides/airfit-f40-review/) is the closest ResMed equivalent; see [Evora Full vs AirFit F40](/us/guides/evora-full-vs-airfit-f40/). For a traditional over-the-nose seal, see the [Vitera](/us/guides/fp-vitera-review/).

## Verdict

The F&P Evora Full is a compact full-face mask that seals under the nose and around the mouth, rated 4–30 cmH2O with 28.2 dBA sound power per F&P. It comes in XS, S-M and L seals with Standard or XL headgear. It cost $149 complete at CPAP.com on 13 October 2026, with $54 replacement cushions.
