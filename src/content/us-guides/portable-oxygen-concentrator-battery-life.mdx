Battery life is the number that decides whether a portable oxygen concentrator (POC) works for your day. Here are the manufacturers' own figures, by setting, for three popular pulse-dose models. All are "up to" figures for a new battery. Real runtime varies with breathing rate, temperature and battery age.

## What drains a battery faster

- **Higher settings.** On the Rove 6 standard battery, going from setting 1 to setting 5 cuts runtime from 6:15 to 1:45.
- **Temperature.** Inogen and OxyGo say to avoid running below 41°F (5°C) or above 95°F (35°C) for long periods to extend runtime.
- **Battery age.** Ratings are for a new battery. Cycle life is 500 (Rove 6, NEXT) or 300 (FreeStyle Comfort).
- **Breathing rate.** CAIRE says FreeStyle Comfort durations stay constant regardless of breath rate. OxyGo's figures assume 17 breaths a minute.

## Battery care (from the manuals)

| Tip | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Store cool and dry at 40–50% charge | Inogen, OxyGo |
| Fully charge and fully discharge at least once every 90 days (Rove 6) | Inogen |
| Don't leave batteries unused more than 90 days; label and rotate several | OxyGo |
| Keep liquids away; stop using a wet battery | Inogen, OxyGo |
| Use AC power whenever available to keep the battery full | Inogen |

## Batteries for flying

All three manuals say to carry enough charged batteries for **at least 150%** of your expected travel time: flight time, ground time, security, connections and delays. CAIRE says most airlines require this. Spare batteries must be individually protected against short circuits and carried in the cabin. Inogen notes that its AC power supply can't charge the battery on board. Worked example: a 4-hour trip door to door needs 6 hours of battery. On a Rove 6 at setting 2, that's two standard batteries (5 h each) or one extended battery (10:15). Full rules: [flying with a portable oxygen concentrator](/us/guides/faa-portable-oxygen-concentrator-rules/).

## Verdict

At low settings, all three will get you through a morning on one standard battery. For a full day, buy the extended or double battery. The Rove 6 extended (up to 12:45 at setting 1) has the longest listed runtime of the three. Above setting 3, expect only 2 to 3 hours per standard battery, so plan for spares or a car charger.
