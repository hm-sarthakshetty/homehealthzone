## Climate Control: Auto vs Manual

| Setting | What ResMed says it does |
| --- | --- |
| Auto (default, recommended) | Tube at 80°F (adjustable 60–86°F or off); humidity held at 85% relative humidity; protects against rainout |
| Manual | Set temperature and humidity separately; rainout protection isn't guaranteed. If rainout happens, raise the tube temperature first |

## Humidity output by tubing (AirSense 11 guide)

| Tubing and setting | Relative humidity at 72°F room | Absolute humidity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SlimLine or standard, humidity 4 (default) | 80% | 6 mg/L or more |
| SlimLine or standard, humidity 8 (max) | 100% | Over 12 mg/L |
| ClimateLineAir 11, Climate Control Auto | 85% | 12 mg/L or more |

So heated tubing in Auto gives humidity comparable to the top unheated setting, while warming the tube to reduce condensation.

## Troubleshooting from ResMed's guide

| Problem | ResMed's suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| Dry or blocked nose | Increase the humidity level |
| Water droplets on your nose, in the mask or tubing | Decrease humidity, or increase tube temperature |
| Very dry mouth | Air may be escaping through your mouth; consider a chin strap or full-face mask |

## Verdict

If you use an AirSense 11 or AirCurve 11 with the humidifier and wake to water in the tube or mask, ClimateLineAir 11 is the documented fix, for $12 more than SlimLine at ResMed's prices. If you don't use the humidifier or never get rainout, SlimLine is cheaper. Tubing has a 90-day ResMed warranty, so plan regular replacements ([CPAP supplies schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/)).
