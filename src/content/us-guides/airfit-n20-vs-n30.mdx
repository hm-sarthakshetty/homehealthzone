## Magnet safety (N20)

ResMed says to keep the N20's magnets at least 2 in (50 mm) away from active medical implants such as pacemakers or defibrillators. Masks with magnetic parts are contraindicated if you have a metallic aneurysm clip or metal splinters in one or both eyes. If that applies to you or your bed partner, the N30 is the magnet-free option of the two.

## Verdict

At the same eShop price, the choice comes down to pressure and fit style. The N20 handles higher pressures and seals over the nose. The N30 is minimal and magnet-free but capped at 20 cmH2O.
