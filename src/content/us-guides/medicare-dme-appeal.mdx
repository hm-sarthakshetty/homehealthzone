This is general information, not legal advice. Medicare Advantage plans have their own appeal process.

## The 5 levels

| Level | Who decides | Deadline to ask | Decision time | 2026 minimum amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Redetermination | Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) | Date on your MSN (120 days on the sample DME MSN) | Generally 60 days | None |
| 2. Reconsideration | Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC) | 180 days after the MAC decision or MSN | 60 days | None |
| 3. Hearing or record review | Office of Medicare Hearings and Appeals (ALJ) | 60 days after the QIC decision | Not stated | $200 |
| 4. Review | Medicare Appeals Council | 60 days after the ALJ decision | Not stated | None stated |
| 5. Judicial review | Federal district court | 60 days after the Council decision | Not stated | $1,960 |

## Filing a level 1 appeal

1. Find the denied item on your [Medicare Summary Notice](/us/guides/medicare-summary-notice-dme/).
2. Either fill out the Redetermination Request Form, or circle the item on a copy of your MSN and explain in writing why you disagree.
3. Include your name, address and Medicare Number, the items and dates, why they should be covered, and supporting information (for example a doctor's note).
4. Mail it to the address on the last page of your MSN.

Before you start, Medicare.gov suggests asking your supplier for information that could make your appeal stronger. If the denial was due to missing paperwork, the supplier may be able to resubmit the claim.

## Common CPAP and oxygen denial reasons to check

Look at the MSN notes and check them against the coverage rules: CPAP adherence after the trial period ([Medicare CPAP rules](/us/guides/medicare-cpap-coverage-compliance/)), oxygen qualifying tests ([Medicare oxygen coverage](/us/guides/medicare-oxygen-coverage/)) and resupply frequency ([CPAP supplies schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/)).

## Verdict

File on time, attach your doctor's records and keep copies of everything.
