## El Paso temperatures (airport, 1991–2020)

| Month | Normal high | Normal low |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May | 88.7°F | 62.1°F |
| June | 97.1°F | 70.6°F |
| July | 95.8°F | 73.0°F |
| August | 94.0°F | 71.8°F |
| September | 88.3°F | 65.4°F |

Station elevation: 1,194.2 m (about 3,918 ft).

Winter normal lows (34.5°F in January) are below the 41°F minimum operating temperature in most manuals, so keep devices in heated rooms in winter too.

## Practical steps

| Situation | Guidance from the documents |
| --- | --- |
| CPAP at night | Normal summer lows are well inside the 95°F limit; use air conditioning |
| POC outdoors at midday | Keep within the 104°F operating limit; seek shade and cool breaks |
| Device left in a car | Storage ranges go to 158°F (AirSense 11, Rove 6); operation doesn't |
| Dry air | Within humidity ranges; see [CPAP dry mouth](/us/guides/cpap-dry-mouth/) |

## Verdict

El Paso's summer daytime heat exceeds CPAP operating limits, so keep equipment air-conditioned and plan outdoor oxygen use around the hottest hours.
