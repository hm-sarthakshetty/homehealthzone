## Cost over a year

The foam cushion's monthly replacement adds up. On ResMed's eShop on 13 October 2026, an AirTouch N20 cushion was $37 and an AirFit N20 cushion $34. Replacing the foam cushion monthly at that price is $444 a year. Check what your insurer allows: see [CPAP supplies replacement schedule](/us/guides/cpap-supplies-replacement-schedule/).

## Who should pick which

| You... | Better fit |
| --- | --- |
| Prefer the feel of foam against your skin | AirTouch N20 |
| Want to wash the cushion daily | AirFit N20 |
| Want lower running costs | AirFit N20 |
| Have a magnet-sensitive implant | Neither; see [AirFit N20 vs N30](/us/guides/airfit-n20-vs-n30/) |

Because the cushions are interchangeable, you can buy one frame and try both.

## Verdict

These are one mask with two cushion options. Start with the silicone AirFit N20 for easy cleaning and lower cost; switch to the AirTouch foam cushion if you prefer foam, and budget for monthly replacements.
