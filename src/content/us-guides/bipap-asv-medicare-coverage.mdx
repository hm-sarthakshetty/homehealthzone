Bilevel (often called BiPAP) and adaptive servo-ventilation (ASV) are prescribed for specific conditions, and Medicare's rules depend on which condition you have. This guide summarizes the two CMS policies: [LCD L33718](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33718) for obstructive sleep apnea and [LCD L33800, Respiratory Assist Devices](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33800) for everything else. It isn't a substitute for your supplier's or practitioner's reading of the policy.

## The codes

| HCPCS | Device | Covered for OSA? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E0601 | CPAP (including auto-CPAP) | Yes, when criteria are met |
| E0470 | Bilevel **without** backup rate | Only after E0601 tried and proven ineffective (L33718) |
| E0471 | Bilevel **with** backup rate | No (L33718) |

## Obstructive sleep apnea: CPAP first

Under [L33718](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33718), Medicare covers E0470 for OSA only after CPAP has been tried and proven ineffective. This matches the AASM's 2019 guidance, which suggests CPAP or APAP over bilevel for routine OSA ([AASM 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6374094/)). See [APAP vs CPAP vs BiPAP](/us/guides/apap-vs-cpap-vs-bipap/) and the [Medicare CPAP guide](/us/guides/medicare-cpap-coverage-compliance/).

## Other conditions: LCD L33800

L33800 (latest revision listed as effective 9 June 2025) covers E0470 and E0471 for restrictive thoracic disorders, central sleep apnea (CSA) or complex sleep apnea (CompSA), hypoventilation syndrome, and chronic respiratory failure consequent to COPD.

| Condition | Key initial criteria (summary of L33800) | Device |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restrictive thoracic disorders (neuromuscular disease such as ALS, or severe thoracic cage abnormality) | Documented diagnosis; **and** awake PaCO₂ ≥ 45 mm Hg, **or** sleep oximetry SpO₂ ≤ 88% for ≥ 5 minutes (2-hour minimum recording), **or** for neuromuscular disease only, maximal inspiratory pressure under 60 cmH₂O or FVC under 50% predicted; **and** COPD doesn't contribute significantly | E0470 or E0471 (practitioner's choice) |
| Central or complex sleep apnea | Complete facility-based, attended PSG documenting CSA/CompSA **and** significant improvement on the home settings, on your prescribed oxygen | E0470 or E0471 (practitioner's choice) |
| Hypoventilation syndrome | Awake PaCO₂ ≥ 45 mm Hg **and** FEV1/FVC ≥ 70%, **plus** either PaCO₂ worsening ≥ 7 mm Hg during or right after sleep, **or** SpO₂ ≤ 88% for ≥ 5 minutes not caused by obstructive events (AHI under 5) | E0470 first; E0471 only with further criteria while on E0470 |
| Chronic respiratory failure from COPD | Separate criteria in L33800 | See the LCD |

Source: [CMS LCD L33800](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33800), read 13 October 2026. If the criteria aren't met, the LCD says the device and accessories will be denied as not reasonable and necessary.

## Months 1–3, then the 61-day re-evaluation

For restrictive thoracic disorders, CSA/CompSA and hypoventilation, coverage starts with the **first three months**. To continue, L33800 requires:

1. A re-evaluation by the treating practitioner **no sooner than 61 days** after starting therapy.
2. Documentation of how symptoms are progressing and how you're using the device.
3. Use averaging **4 hours per 24 hours** by the time of the re-evaluation. Less is treated as non-compliant use and is reason to deny continued coverage.
4. A signed, dated practitioner statement, made no sooner than 61 days in, that you're using the device compliantly and benefiting.

Medicare won't pay for month 4 onward until this is done.

## ASV: who it is and isn't for

ASV is prescribed for central sleep apnea patterns. The safety warnings matter:

- ResMed's [AirCurve 10 ASV user guide](https://document.resmed.com/documents/products/machine/aircurve-series/user-guide/aircurve-10-asv_user-guide_amer_eng.pdf) says ASV therapy **is contraindicated** in patients with chronic, symptomatic heart failure (NYHA 2–4) with reduced left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF ≤ 45%) and moderate to severe predominant central sleep apnea.
- The AASM's updated recommendation says ASV targeted to normalize AHI **should not be used** for CHF-related central sleep apnea in adults with LVEF ≤ 45% and moderate or severe CSA-predominant sleep-disordered breathing, because of evidence of an increased risk of death. It **can be used** with LVEF above 45% or mild CHF-related CSA ([AASM ASV update](https://aasm.org/resources/practiceparameters/asv.pdf)).
- The AASM's safety notice advises assessing every patient for heart failure before ASV, and measuring LVEF objectively if symptoms are present ([AASM safety notice](https://aasm.org/special-safety-notice-asv-therapy-for-central-sleep-apnea-patients-with-heart-failure/)).

The AirCurve 10 ASV guide lists a maximum supplemental oxygen flow of 15 L/min in CPAP and ASV modes and 4 L/min in ASVAuto ([AirCurve 10 ASV user guide](https://document.resmed.com/documents/products/machine/aircurve-series/user-guide/aircurve-10-asv_user-guide_amer_eng.pdf)). See [CPAP with oxygen](/us/guides/cpap-with-oxygen/).

## Checklist before you order

| Step | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Confirm the diagnosis code and condition category | Decides which LCD applies |
| Make sure the required test is done (PSG, ABG, oximetry, spirometry) | The criteria are test-based |
| Ask whether E0470 or E0471 is being ordered and why | E0471 isn't covered for OSA |
| Diary the 61-day re-evaluation | Coverage for month 4 onward depends on it |
| Use the device at least 4 hours a night on average | Below that counts as non-compliant |
| For ASV: get an LVEF assessment if you have heart failure symptoms | Contraindication and AASM guidance |

## Verdict

For obstructive sleep apnea, Medicare wants CPAP tried first, and bilevel with a backup rate isn't covered. For other conditions, the criteria are test-driven, with a 61-day check before month 4. ASV can help some central sleep apnea patients, but it must not be used in symptomatic heart failure with LVEF ≤ 45%. Make sure that's been checked.
