## Running cost

At 285 W for 24 hours a day, the Companion 5 uses about 6.8 kWh a day (285 W x 24 h), or roughly 208 kWh in a 30.4-day month. Multiply by your utility's rate; our [electricity cost guide](/us/guides/oxygen-concentrator-electricity-cost/) uses EIA averages.

## Safety points from the manual

- Keep at least 12 in of clearance around the unit.
- Don't use extension cords.
- No smoking or open flames near oxygen ([home oxygen fire safety](/us/guides/home-oxygen-fire-safety/)).

## Verdict

From the documents, the Companion 5 is a lighter, lower-power choice for anyone prescribed 5 LPM or less, with a low 0.5 LPM minimum and a high altitude rating. If your prescription could rise above 5 LPM, look at a 10 LPM model instead.
