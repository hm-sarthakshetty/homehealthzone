## Philips Oxygenate 5 manufacturer and country of origin

**Oxygenate 5 is manufactured by Jiangsu Konsung in China.** A Philips technical sheet explicitly identifies China as the country of production, Konsung as manufacturer and KSW-5 as the manufacturer model code. Philips' brochure separately names Konsung as manufacturer and Philips as distributor. These records identify the manufacturer; they do not disclose how engineering work was divided between the companies. [Philips technical sheet, page 1](https://www.elettromedicali.it/wp-content/plugins/ant-webcommerce/global/files/20-05-2025-14-55-34.pdf?x89244=#page=1) · [Philips brochure, page 8](https://medidyne.se/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/ENG-Philips-Oxygenate-5-Brochure.pdf#page=8).

Philips distributes the Oxygenate 5. Philips India's Oxygenate 5 page uses the corresponding product number. This supports the KSW-5 model relationship; it does not establish that every KSW-5 sold under a different label has the same regional accessories, warranty or configuration. ## Philips Oxygenate 5 specifications

Philips lists continuous flow of **0.5–5 L/min**, oxygen concentration of **93% ±3%**, **18 kg** weight and sound **below 40 dB(A)**. Its India product page lists **230 V / 50 Hz** and a power rating below **360 VA**. VA is an apparent-power rating; it should not be treated as a measured electricity consumption in watts. The listed sound figure is a manufacturer specification and can vary with operating conditions.

The India launch describes a digital flow display and nine levels of filtration. Choose and operate a concentrator at the flow prescribed by a qualified clinician.

**HHZ's Oxygenate 5 budgeting estimate is ₹45,000–₹55,000; this is not a verified range of seller offers.** One retailer listing at ₹45,000 was checked on 3 November 2026. The ₹55,000 upper budget is not a verified seller quote. Obtain a current itemised offer specifying taxes, configuration, delivery, accessories and service. A historical premium over an India-sold Konsung KSW-5 has not been established. [Single price listing checked](https://respbuy.com/product/philips-oxygenate-5-oxygen-concentrator/).

Philips India describes a **three-year limited device warranty** and a **10,000-hour sieve warranty**. These are separately described terms; ask the seller for the full written conditions, exclusions, start date, repair arrangements and any labour or transport charges. Philips also describes additional replaceable filters in the India package; confirm the contents of the unit being sold.

## Oxygenate 5 and Philips EverFlo

The Oxygenate 5 and EverFlo are different named models. The KSW-5 manufacturer documentation applies to Oxygenate 5; it does not establish shared components, accessories or warranty with EverFlo. See the [Philips EverFlo product reference](/oxygen-concentrators/philips-everflo-5-lpm/) and the [Oxygenate 5 vs Konsung KSW-5 investigation](/guides/philips-oxygenate-5-konsung-ksw-5/) before comparing specific offers.

**Philips Oxygenate 5 has a published nominal weight of 18 kg, compared with the 14 kg Philips EverFlo brochure figure: 4 kg, approximately 28.6%, higher.** Calculation: (18 − 14) ÷ 14 × 100. HHZ has not weighed matched units. Assess the caregiver's handling requirements; the weight difference does not establish injury risk or sieve chemistry. The below-360 VA rating does not establish measured electricity savings. [Philips EverFlo brochure, page 4](https://www.documents.philips.com/doclib/enc/9801469/EverFlo_brochure.pdf#page=4).

## Buying for a hospital, dealer or rental fleet

**HHZ has not audited Oxygenate 5 service coverage or repair costs in Indian cities.** The manual directs owners to Philips Respironics for service facilitation. Request the local provider, warranty issuer, repair location, parts availability, turnaround and full charges in writing. A procurement quote should identify the unit, manufacturer, model, product code, voltage, condition, accessories, taxes and delivery. [Manual service section, PDF page 3](https://www.lungenliga.ch/sites/default/files/documents/philips-oxygenate-5-bedienungsanleitung-en.pdf#page=3).

Ask specifically whether approved PCB modules, valves and sieve columns are available for this model, who performs repairs, and whether the unit must be transported elsewhere. If the seller cannot document a usable repair route and itemised charges, HHZ would not assign extra value to a Philips service promise in the quote.
