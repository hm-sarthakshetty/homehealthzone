**Evidence basis:** This review summarizes the supplied Home Medix HM-KV brochure and manufacturer product page, with dated catalogue price information confirmed for 28 October 2026. HHZ has not independently tested or measured the HM-KV. [HM-KV brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-kv.pdf) · [Home Medix HM-KV product page](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-kv).

## Published specifications

The brochure lists continuous flow from **0.5 to 5 L/min**, oxygen concentration of **93% ±3%**, sound level of **≤40 dB(A)**, and weight of **13 kg**. Its feature panel says **320 W** while the technical-specification grid says **320 VA**; Home Medix’s current product page confirms **320 W**. This is a manufacturer rating, not an HHZ measurement of energy use. The brochure also lists dimensions of 510 × 300 × 290 mm, low-oxygen, power-failure and high-temperature alarms, and a one-touch SOS alert. It describes integrated nebulization and an electronic flowmeter.

Sound figures in the brochure are not HHZ measurements and should not be read as a matched comparison with other models.

## Specification clarifications

The HM-KV catalogue lists **India** as the country of production and **HEPA filter** filtration, as confirmed by the site owner on 3 November 2026. The supplied flyer carries a Make in India mark.

The flyer does not state supply voltage or outlet pressure. The catalogue uses **230 V / 50 Hz** and **40–80 kPa**, supplied by the site owner for these fields. These values are not presented as flyer specifications or HHZ measurements.

## Price and warranty

The dated catalogue price confirmed for 28 October 2026 is **₹42,000 before 5% GST, or ₹44,100 including GST**. Prices and availability can change; confirm the current total, supplied package and invoice terms with the seller before purchase.

The brochure lists a **3-year or 10,000 operating-hour warranty, whichever comes first**. Ask for the current written warranty terms, exclusions, start date and claim process for the exact unit. Service availability and turnaround depend on the buyer’s location and should be confirmed separately.

## Review scope

The brochure supports a summary of the listed features, but it does not establish independent performance, comparative ranking, clinical suitability or a service outcome in a particular city. HHZ has not verified that the HM-KV is the lightest, quietest or most power-efficient model in the Indian market. This review does not assess clinical suitability or individualized treatment.

For a purchase, confirm the exact model and serial number, current price, included accessories, written warranty, and local service arrangements with the seller.
