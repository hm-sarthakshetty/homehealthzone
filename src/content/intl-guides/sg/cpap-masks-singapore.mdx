Prices are in Singapore dollars, read from each retailer's site on 14 October 2026. Each retailer's own product page is linked. Some listings were out of stock; those are marked.

## Pick the type first

- **Full-face**: covers nose and mouth. For mouth breathers.
- **Nasal or cradle**: sits over or under the nose.
- **Nasal pillows**: small cushions that sit in the nostrils. The lightest option.

Comooz sells any ResMed nasal, pillows or full-face mask for a flat **S$240**, or **S$188** when bought with a machine. That makes it a useful ceiling when comparing.
