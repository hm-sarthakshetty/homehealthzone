## Singapore's climate in numbers

The Meteorological Service Singapore's [climate summary](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-climate-of-singapore/) describes a climate that barely changes through the year:

- **Humidity:** more than 90% in the early morning before sunrise, falling to around 60% on dry afternoons. The annual mean is about **82%**, and it often reaches 100% during long spells of rain.
- **Temperature:** nights usually stay at 23 to 25°C; days peak at 31 to 33°C. May is the warmest month, with a 24-hour mean of 28.6°C.
- **Rain:** it rains on an average of 171 days a year.

For a CPAP user the important gap is between the warm, humid air outside and the cool, air-conditioned bedroom where the tube runs.

## Why water collects in the tube

The humidifier warms and moistens the air. As that air travels down the hose it cools towards room temperature, and the moisture condenses into droplets, which is called rainout. The bigger the gap between the humidified air and a cold room, the more water you get. Air-conditioning blowing directly on the tube makes it worse. Our [rainout explainer](/clinical/cpap-rainout-water-in-hose/) covers the physics in detail.

## ResMed's settings

From ResMed's [AirSense 11 user guide](https://document.resmed.com/documents/products/machine/airsense-11/user-guide/airsense11_user-guide_amer_mul.pdf):

| Problem | ResMed's suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| Dry or blocked nose | Increase the humidity level |
| Water droplets on your nose, in the mask or tubing | Decrease humidity, or increase tube temperature |
| Very dry mouth | Air may be escaping through your mouth; consider a chin strap or full-face mask |

With a ClimateLineAir heated tube and HumidAir tub on an AirSense 11, **Climate Control Auto** is the default. ResMed says it sets the tube to 27°C (80°F), holds 85% relative humidity and is designed to prevent rainout. Without a heated tube you can only change the humidity level, so you cannot raise the tube temperature.

Other practical steps:

- Keep the machine lower than your head, so water runs back rather than into the mask.
- Point the air-conditioner away from the bed and the hose.
- Use the cleanable water tub with distilled water if your machine's guide calls for it, and empty it each morning.
