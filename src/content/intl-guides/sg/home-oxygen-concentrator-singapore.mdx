**5 or 10 L/min?** Your prescription decides. A 10 L/min machine is for patients prescribed higher flows. In the table above, the 5 L/min models are lighter, and the 8F-5A's rated input is less than half the 7F-10's.

## Running costs at Singapore's tariff

SP Group's [regulated household tariff](https://www.spgroup.com.sg/our-services/utilities/tariff-information) for 1 October to 31 December 2026 is **31.16 cents/kWh including GST** (28.59 cents before GST).

A rating in volt-amperes (VA) is the maximum the machine draws, and the real power used is usually lower, so these are ceilings:

| Rated input | 24 hours a day | 30 days, 24 h | 30 days, 8 h a night |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 350 VA (Yuwell 8F-5A) | up to S$2.62 | up to S$78.52 | up to S$26.17 |
| 850 VA (Yuwell 7F-10) | up to S$6.36 | up to S$190.70 | up to S$63.57 |

SP Group sets the tariff quarterly, so recalculate when the next quarter's rate is published.

## Power and plugs

Singapore runs on **230 V, 50 Hz** with **Type G** three-pin sockets, the same as the UK ([Electrical Safety First](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/guidance/advice-for-you/when-travelling/travel-adaptor-for-singapore/)). Fu Kang Healthcare lists the Yuwell 7F-5's supply as 220 V ±22 V, 50 Hz ±1 Hz, and says it has a power-cut warning function. Check the plate on any machine bought overseas before plugging it in.

## Paying less

- **Seniors 60+:** AIC's Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund pays up to 90%, capped per device. You must be approved before buying. See [oxygen concentrator subsidies](/sg/guides/oxygen-concentrator-subsidy-singapore/).
- **Serious illness, under a year's prognosis:** AIC's Equipment Rental Scheme pays up to 80% of weekly rental.
- **Under 60 with a disability:** SG Enable's Assistive Technology Fund.

## Safety and paperwork

- Bion states an oxygen concentrator is a medical device that needs a doctor's prescription.
- Medical devices supplied in Singapore are regulated by the Health Sciences Authority. Bringing one in from abroad needs HSA approval; see [HSA medical device rules](/sg/guides/hsa-medical-device-rules-singapore/).
- For a machine you can carry and fly with, see [portable oxygen concentrators in Singapore](/sg/guides/portable-oxygen-concentrator-singapore/).
