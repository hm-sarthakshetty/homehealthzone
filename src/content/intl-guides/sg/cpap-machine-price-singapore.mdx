All prices are in Singapore dollars and were read from each retailer's own website on 14 October 2026. Comooz states its prices are nett and include GST. Check the retailer's page before you buy, because prices and promotions change.

## Check the subsidy first

Since April 2026, people aged 16+ with moderate-to-severe sleep apnoea who pass a one-month adherence trial can get a **one-time government subsidy** for the ResMed AirSense 10 AutoSet. See [CPAP subsidy in Singapore](/sg/guides/cpap-subsidy-singapore/). It is the only model on the list, so it changes the maths for anyone eligible.

Comooz says the price differences in Singapore come down to three things: whether the machine is auto-adjusting, whether a mask and humidifier are bundled, and whether setup, fitting and follow-up are charged separately. Its advice is to compare the total of machine, mask and setup, not the headline price.

| AirSense 11, 1 month | S$125, plus S$240 mask and S$360 refundable deposit |
| 5-night home trial | S$240 mask (yours to keep) + S$360 refundable deposit; S$98 processing fee if you return the machine |

Rent paid in the first three months is offset against the purchase price. Comooz works out that after a 5-night trial, the balance to buy is S$1,268 for the AirSense 10 bundle and S$1,468 for the AirSense 11 bundle. By its own figures, buying is cheaper than renting beyond roughly six months of nightly use.

## What is included

- **Medbitz** lists ResMed machines with a ClimateLineAir heated tube, a filter, the power adaptor, a travel bag and a two-year manufacturer's warranty. It includes a consultation with a Registered Polysomnographic Technologist (RPSGT).
- **Yes CPAP** lists the iBreeze Auto with heated humidifier, heated tube, SD card, a two-year warranty and a power brick with a Singapore/UK three-pin plug rated 100 to 240 V.
- **Comooz** says delivery, setup, mask fitting and follow-up are free.

## Before you buy

- **Prescription.** Your pressure settings come from your doctor.
- **Humidity.** Singapore's air is humid all year. If water collects in your tube, see [CPAP in Singapore's humidity](/sg/guides/cpap-humidity-singapore/).
- **Running costs.** Masks and parts are recurring costs; see [CPAP masks](/sg/guides/cpap-masks-singapore/) and [CPAP tubes and filters](/sg/guides/cpap-humidity-singapore/).
- **Bringing one in from abroad.** Personal imports need HSA approval; see [HSA medical device rules](/sg/guides/hsa-medical-device-rules-singapore/).
