This guide is written from Health NZ’s [long-term CPAP therapy in Canterbury Waitaha](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/hospitals-services/hospitals/canterbury/respiratory-services-canterbury/long-term-cpap-therapy-in-canterbury-waitaha) page, read on 14 October 2026. Each region runs its own sleep service, so ask yours about local rules.

## Canterbury example

- After a **successful CPAP trial** through Christchurch Hospital Sleep Health Services, **you keep the machine** to use long term.
- Sleep Health Services sets the pressure. Use CPAP every time you sleep. **If you are not using it, you will be asked to return it.**
- Take the machine on holiday and into hospital with you.

## Masks

If you have hospital-funded CPAP, you are eligible for **one replacement mask a year**. More often than that, you may need to pay. Health NZ names USL Medical, Cansleep and Sleep Well Clinic as places to buy masks. For retail mask prices, see [CPAP masks in New Zealand](/nz/guides/cpap-masks-nz/). They cost $229–$300 on 14 October 2026.

## Driving and pilot checks

Some people need a medical review under **NZTA** (driving) or **CAA** (aviation) guidelines, which may include checking CPAP usage. Health NZ says these people are given a **cloud-connected** machine and asked to download a monitoring app.
