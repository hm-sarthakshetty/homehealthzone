This guide is written from Health NZ’s [long-term CPAP therapy in Canterbury Waitaha](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/hospitals-services/hospitals/canterbury/respiratory-services-canterbury/long-term-cpap-therapy-in-canterbury-waitaha) page, read on 14 October 2026. Follow your own sleep service’s and manufacturer’s instructions if they differ.

## Cleaning schedule

| When | Part | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Mask seal | Wash in warm soapy water; dishwashing detergent is best |
| Daily | Water chamber | Remove from the machine, rinse and refill |
| Weekly | Water chamber | Wash in warm soapy water, rinse, dry; soak 1 hour in 1 part white vinegar to 4 parts warm water |
| Weekly | Hose | Rinse and leave to dry |
| Weekly | Headgear | Machine-wash in a wash bag or pillowcase |
| Monthly | Filter | Check it and brush off dust |

Avoid very hot water, harsh chemicals and sunlight, which damage seals and plastic. Keep the mask away from children and pets. Only your sleep service should change the pressure.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What Health NZ suggests |
| --- | --- |
| Water building up in the tube or mask | Turn humidity down one setting at a time |
| Dry nose or mouth | Turn humidity up one setting at a time, or try a full face mask |
| Machine blowing cold air | Warm the room |
| Mask leaking around the seal | Refit and adjust the straps; practise in front of a mirror; clean the mask |
| Noisy hose | Air is probably escaping; check for holes and connections |
| Sore eyes | Reposition the mask to stop air leaking towards your eyes |
| Pressure too high to fall asleep | Use the ramp feature if your machine has one |
| Still snoring with CPAP | The pressure may need changing; contact your sleep service |
| Machine not working | Check the wall switch and power cable, and that the filter is clean |

## Power cuts

If the power goes off while you are using CPAP, take the mask off and sleep without it; the vents let you breathe room air. Restart CPAP when power returns. Don’t drive or operate heavy machinery if you are sleepy. If you rely on powered equipment, see [medically dependent consumers](/nz/guides/medically-dependent-power-nz/).

Replacement masks cost $228–$300 in New Zealand; see [CPAP masks in New Zealand](/nz/guides/cpap-masks-nz/).
