## Trials and rentals

ResMed’s NZ shop lists a **CPAP trial of the AirSense 11 AutoSet at $35** and an AirSense 10 Elite rental at $35. The listings don’t state the period, so ask before you book.

New Zealand has no pre-market approval for devices; see [Medsafe and WAND](/nz/guides/medsafe-wand-medical-devices-nz/).
