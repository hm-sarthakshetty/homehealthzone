## Flying with a concentrator

Air New Zealand accepts approved or FAA-labelled POCs with medical clearance; see [flying with CPAP or oxygen](/nz/guides/flying-with-cpap-oxygen-nz/).

## Regulation

New Zealand has no pre-market approval for devices, only WAND notification; see [Medsafe and WAND](/nz/guides/medsafe-wand-medical-devices-nz/).
