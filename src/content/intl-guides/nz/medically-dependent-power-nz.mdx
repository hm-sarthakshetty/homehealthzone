This guide is written from the Electricity Authority’s page [if you rely on electricity for medical reasons](https://www.ea.govt.nz/your-power/consumer-care-obligations/if-you-rely-on-electricity-for-medical-reasons/), read on 14 October 2026.

## Register with your power company

If you, or someone in your home, relies on electricity to prevent serious harm to health (for example a home oxygen concentrator), **tell your power company**. It must help you register as a **medically dependent consumer**.

Your power company may ask a health practitioner to confirm this. Take the **Confirmation of Medically Dependent Consumer Status** form and your emergency plan to your doctor so they can sign the form and help with the plan.

## Protection from disconnection

Your power company **can’t disconnect you** if it knows you or someone in your household:

- is registered as a medically dependent consumer
- is applying to register
- is disputing the power company’s decision on your status

You still have to **pay your bills**. The Authority advises choosing a **postpay** plan.

## Power cuts still happen

Even if you are medically dependent, a constant supply **can’t be guaranteed**. The Authority says to:

- get an emergency response plan template from your power company
- make the plan with your healthcare providers and support people, and share it
- talk to your doctor about **backup power** for medical equipment

For CPAP, Health NZ Canterbury says that if the power goes off during the night, take the mask off and sleep without CPAP; the mask vents let you breathe room air. Restart when the power returns. See [CPAP cleaning and troubleshooting](/nz/guides/cpap-cleaning-troubleshooting-nz/).

For home oxygen funding, see [funded home oxygen](/nz/guides/home-oxygen-funding-new-zealand/).
