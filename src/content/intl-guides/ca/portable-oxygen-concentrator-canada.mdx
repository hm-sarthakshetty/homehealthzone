## Flying

Air Canada and WestJet have different battery and approval rules. See [flying with CPAP or oxygen in Canada](/ca/guides/flying-with-cpap-oxygen-canada/).

## Funding

Provinces fund home oxygen through their own programmes, usually through a contracted vendor rather than a retail purchase. See [Ontario ADP home oxygen](/ca/guides/ontario-adp-home-oxygen/), [BC Home Oxygen Program](/ca/guides/bc-home-oxygen-program/) and [Alberta AADL](/ca/guides/alberta-aadl-home-oxygen/).
