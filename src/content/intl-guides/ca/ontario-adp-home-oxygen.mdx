This guide is written from Ontario’s [home oxygen therapy page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-oxygen-therapy), last updated 10 July 2025 and read on 14 October 2026. It is not medical advice.

## Who qualifies

- Ontario resident with a valid health card
- Meets ADP’s medical criteria for home oxygen, such as **hypoxemia (low blood oxygen) at rest**
- Income is not considered

## What is covered

ADP funds the oxygen itself and the equipment and supplies to use it (concentrators or tanks, tubing and masks), plus delivery, set-up and maintenance. The vendor maintains, repairs and replaces the equipment, unless it was damaged by misuse.

## Who pays what

| Situation | ADP pays | You pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most adults | 75% | 25% |
| Aged 65 or older | 100% | Nothing |
| Living in a long-term care home | 100% | Nothing |
| On Ontario Works, ODSP or ACSD | 100% | Nothing |
| Receiving professional services through Ontario Health atHome | 100% | Nothing |

## How to apply

1. Your doctor or nurse practitioner arranges the tests that show you meet the criteria.
2. They complete the application form.
3. You choose an ADP-registered home oxygen business.
4. The business completes its part of the form.
5. The business submits the application electronically.

Funding must be **renewed every year**. The vendor arranges the testing.

## Buying a concentrator privately

You can buy a concentrator yourself, for example a portable unit for travel, but ADP funding goes through registered vendors.
