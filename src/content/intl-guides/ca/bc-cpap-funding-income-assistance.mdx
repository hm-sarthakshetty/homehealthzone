This guide is written from the BC government’s [medical equipment and devices policy for service providers](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/income-assistance/access-services/service-providers/medical-equipment-therapies), read on 14 October 2026. Check with the ministry before you buy.

## Who it is for

- People receiving **disability assistance**
- People on **income assistance** who have a life-threatening health need
- Some non-ministry clients who have a life-threatening health need

## Requirements

- **Pre-approval is a legal requirement.** The ministry needs a quote from the supplier.
- **Baseline overnight testing** within the previous six months.
- A **30-day compliance report** showing the machine was used at least **80% of the time**, for at least **4 hours a night**.

## What can be funded

- The CPAP trial and mask fitting
- Purchase of the machine
- Yearly supplies
- Repairs and warranty administration
- Loaner equipment

## Other sources first

The ministry funds equipment only when no other resource can pay. Those resources include Fair PharmaCare, health authorities, ICBC, WorkSafeBC, Veterans Affairs Canada and private insurance.
