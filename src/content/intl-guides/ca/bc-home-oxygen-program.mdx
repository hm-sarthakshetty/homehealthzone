This guide is written from BC’s [COPD guideline Appendix C on home oxygen](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/practitioner-pro/bc-guidelines/copd_appendix_c.pdf) and [Fraser Health’s Home Oxygen Program page](https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/home-oxygen-program), read on 14 October 2026. It is not medical advice.

## Medical criteria (BC guideline)

The guideline notes that criteria may vary between health authorities. In every case you must be on optimal treatment and **not actively smoking**.

| Type | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Resting | PaO2 ≤ 55 mmHg on room air, **or** SpO2 below 88% continuously for 6 minutes |
| Resting with heart failure or pulmonary hypertension | PaO2 ≤ 60 mmHg |
| Walking (ambulatory) | SpO2 below 88% for at least 1 minute while walking on a **flat surface** (not a treadmill) |
| Night-time (nocturnal) | SpO2 below 88% for more than 30% of at least 4 hours of oximetry |

If you can’t walk for one minute, ambulatory oxygen is not funded on that basis. People with sleep apnoea get oxygen only if they meet the nocturnal criteria **despite optimal CPAP**.

## Applying: Fraser Health example

Each health authority runs its own programme. Fraser Health says:

- You need to be a BC resident with Medical Services Plan (MSP) coverage, live in the Fraser Health region, and not be in acute or residential care.
- Your doctor or nurse practitioner sends an application and prescription to **Community Respiratory Services** (fax 604-514-6079). A respirologist decides.
- The programme does not fund you if WorkSafeBC (WCB), ICBC, Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB), the RCMP or Veterans Affairs Canada covers you.
- Palliative patients must also meet the criteria.
- If you are not approved, you can pay privately or use extended health benefits.

## Paying privately

Retail concentrator prices are in [home oxygen concentrators in Canada](/ca/guides/home-oxygen-concentrator-canada/). For CPAP help for people on income or disability assistance in BC, see [BC CPAP funding on income assistance](/ca/guides/bc-cpap-funding-income-assistance/).
