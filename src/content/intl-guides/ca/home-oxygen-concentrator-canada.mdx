## Check the licence

Health Canada’s licence listing includes a Yuwell oxygen concentrator (licence 111620, first licensed 8 August 2024). Search the listing for any other model before you buy; see [Health Canada licensing](/ca/guides/health-canada-licence-cpap-oxygen/).

## Power

Canada uses **120 V, 60 Hz** with **Type A and B** sockets ([Electrical Safety First](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/canada/)). A concentrator bought abroad for 230 V won’t run safely here without the correct rating. Check the label.

## Funded oxygen

If you meet your province’s criteria, the province usually pays a vendor to supply and maintain the concentrator. See [Ontario ADP home oxygen](/ca/guides/ontario-adp-home-oxygen/), [BC Home Oxygen Program](/ca/guides/bc-home-oxygen-program/) and [Alberta AADL](/ca/guides/alberta-aadl-home-oxygen/).
