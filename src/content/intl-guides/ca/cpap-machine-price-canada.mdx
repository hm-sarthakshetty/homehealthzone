All prices are in Canadian dollars, read from each retailer’s store data on 14 October 2026. They exclude shipping, and tax is added at checkout. A prescription is usually needed. In Ontario, ADP pays 75% if you go through a registered sleep clinic and vendor; see [Ontario ADP CPAP funding](/ca/guides/ontario-adp-cpap-funding/).

## Check the licence

Canadian CPAP Store says its AirSense 11 is ResMed’s Canadian model, licensed by Health Canada. The AirSense 11 appears in Health Canada’s licence database as licence 106650. See [Health Canada licensing for CPAP and oxygen](/ca/guides/health-canada-licence-cpap-oxygen/).
