A concentrator or CPAP stops when the power goes off. In the UK, the main protection is the **Priority Services Register (PSR)**, plus a backup plan agreed with your NHS oxygen supplier.

## The Priority Services Register

From [Ofgem](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/your-energy-supply/how-manage-your-energy-supply/join-your-suppliers-priority-services-register), read 14 October 2026:

- **Two registers.** Your energy supplier (the company that bills you) and your network operator (the company responsible for the wires) each keep a PSR. Contact both directly.
- **Who can join.** People who have reached state pension age. People with a disability or long-term medical condition, including those who use medical equipment that needs a power supply.
- **What you get:**
  - priority support in an emergency
  - wherever possible, advance notice of scheduled power cuts
  - an identification and password scheme, so you know a caller or visitor is genuine

The PSR doesn't guarantee your power stays on. It means you're known and prioritised for help.

## NHS home oxygen consent

When NHS home oxygen starts, you sign a Home Oxygen Consent Form. [Air Liquide's prescriber guidance](https://uk.healthcare.airliquide.com/chronic-respiratory-diseases/our-solutions/ordering-home-oxygen) says it covers sharing your details with the oxygen supplier and other parties, including fire and rescue services and electricity distributors. Registering with your own supplier and network operator as well means you're on both lists.

## A power-cut plan

| Equipment | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| NHS oxygen concentrator | Ask your oxygen supplier what backup it provides, such as cylinders, and how to switch over. Suppliers run a 24-hour helpline: [Air Liquide](https://uk.healthcare.airliquide.com/respiratory/our-solutions/home-oxygen-service) lists a 24-hour, 7-day call centre |
| Portable concentrator | Keep batteries charged. Battery life depends on the setting. See [portable concentrators](/uk/guides/best-portable-oxygen-concentrator-uk/) |
| CPAP | Ask your sleep clinic whether you can safely skip a night. For a battery option, check what the manufacturer supports for your model |

## The verdict

Join both Priority Services Registers today. Agree a backup with your oxygen supplier. Keep the supplier's 24-hour number by the concentrator.
