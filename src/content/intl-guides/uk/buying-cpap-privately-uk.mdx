In the UK, [CPAP is free on the NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/obstructive-sleep-apnoea/) if you need it. So most private buyers are after a second machine, a travel machine or a newer model. This guide uses prices from ResMed's own UK shop, read on 14 October 2026.

ResMed shows these prices "plus VAT". A buyer who qualifies for [VAT relief](/uk/guides/vat-relief-cpap-oxygen-uk/) pays the listed figure. Other buyers add 20%.

Published specifications: the AirSense 11 weighs about 1.1 kg with a 4–20 cmH₂O pressure range. The AirMini weighs about 0.3 kg and has no water tub. It uses waterless HumidX humidification instead.

Masks cost about £104–£137 + VAT. See [CPAP masks in the UK](/uk/guides/cpap-masks-uk/).

Electricity costs little. At the Ofgem price cap of [26.32p per kWh](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/information-consumers/energy-advice-households/energy-price-cap-unit-rates-and-standing-charges) (October to December 2026, Direct Debit average), every 100 W drawn for 8 hours costs about 21p. A CPAP's draw rises with humidification and a heated tube.

## AirSense 11 or AirMini?

- **AirSense 11** is the bedside machine. It has a water-tub humidifier option (HumidAir 11) and costs less.
- **AirMini** is for travel. It is very light, but there's no water tub and the bundle price is higher. Buy one only if you travel often. The CAA says airlines don't guarantee in-flight power, so check before you fly (see [flying with CPAP or oxygen](/uk/guides/flying-with-cpap-oxygen-uk/)).

## Before you buy

- **Check the NHS route.** It is free if you need CPAP. Use our [NHS CPAP guide](/uk/guides/nhs-cpap-sleep-apnoea/).
- **Use your prescribed pressure.** Ask your sleep clinic for your settings, or whether to use auto mode.
- **Claim VAT relief at checkout, not afterwards.** ResMed's shop says it can't refund VAT paid in error.

## The verdict

For a private bedside machine, the AirSense 11 AutoSet at £669 + VAT is the default. Buy an AirMini bundle (from £820 + VAT) only as a travel second unit. Either way, claim VAT relief if you have sleep apnoea.
