## How to choose

1. **Mouth breathing or a dry mouth in the morning?** Start with full face. The F20 is the standard choice; the F40 and F30i are more minimal.
2. **Nose breather?** Pillows (P10, P30i) feel the lightest. A nasal cushion (N20, N30i) suits people who find pillows irritating.
3. **Sleep on your side or move around?** Top-of-head tube designs (F30i, N30i, P30i) keep the hose out of the way.
4. **Sizing.** Most of these masks come in several cushion sizes, so use the manufacturer's fitting guide.

## The verdict

If you're on NHS CPAP, ask your sleep clinic about replacement masks first. If you buy privately, the F20 (£129 + VAT) and P10 (£107.50 + VAT) are the safest starting points for mouth and nose breathers. Replacing a cushion or headgear is often much cheaper than buying a new mask.
