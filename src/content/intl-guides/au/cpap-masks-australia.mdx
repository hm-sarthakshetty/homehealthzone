## Spare parts cost less than a mask

cpap.com.au sells spares such as the Philips DreamWear silicone pillow cushion for $89, the F&P Brevida pillow seal for $65 and F&P Solo pillows for $75. ResMed's shop lists the AirFit F40 QuietAir vent at $12.95. Purpose-designed spare parts for a GST-free item are GST-free too.

## Who pays

State programs often supply the machine but not the mask. EnableNSW, for example, expects Home Respiratory Program clients to buy CPAP masks themselves ([EnableNSW guide](/au/guides/enablensw-home-respiratory-program/)). ResMed Australia says some private health funds contribute to masks.
