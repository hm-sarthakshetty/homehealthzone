This guide is written from EnableNSW's [Home Respiratory Program fact sheet](https://www.enable.health.nsw.gov.au/about/publications/fact-sheets/home-respiratory-program) and its [eligibility and financial criteria](https://enable.health.nsw.gov.au/for_individuals/applying-to-EnableNSW/eligibility-and-financial-criteria) page. EnableNSW's site blocks scripted access, so we read archived 2026 copies of both pages; check the live pages before applying.

## What the program supplies

The Home Respiratory Program (HRP) provides:

- home oxygen equipment, typically an oxygen concentrator
- continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) and other respiratory support devices
- ventilators
- some respiratory consumables

Each product has its own clinical criteria, and a prescriber applies on your behalf.

**Priority and waiting.** EnableNSW funds the primary oxygen equipment used at home first. Waiting periods vary for CPAP, respiratory support devices, ventilators and consumables. Equipment is bought and supplied "as funding becomes available".

## Who is eligible

You must:

- live in NSW or on Lord Howe Island, or be an asylum seeker, refugee or humanitarian visa holder living there
- be enrolled with Medicare
- not be receiving, or eligible for, the equipment through insurance or another government scheme, such as the NDIS

## The co-payment

EnableNSW charges a co-payment each financial year you receive equipment, consumable re-orders or repairs.

| Band | Who | Co-payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Adults on a full pension; children under 16 | $100 a financial year |
| 2 | Taxable income up to $42,000 (single) or $70,000 (couple), plus $2,100 per dependant | $100 a financial year |
| 3 | Taxable income above those limits | 20% of equipment costing $800 or more |

Band 3 consumers are **not eligible** for equipment that costs under $800. EnableNSW says its income bands come from the former Program for Appliances for Disabled People, adjusted for inflation since December 1998.

Short-term oxygen therapy has **no co-payment**.

### What 20% means in dollars

For a Band 3 adult, 20% of an oxygen concentrator listed at $999 (the Philips EverFlo at Air Liquide's online store on 14 October 2026) would be about $200, and 20% of a $1,495 auto-CPAP would be $299. EnableNSW's own equipment costs may differ from retail.

## Your responsibilities

To keep HRP equipment you must:

- have stopped smoking for at least **four weeks**
- follow the supplier's safety precautions
- use the therapy as prescribed, including the hours of use
- buy some products and accessories yourself, **such as CPAP masks**
- return the device when you no longer need it or are not using it as prescribed

For mask prices, see [CPAP masks in Australia](/au/guides/cpap-masks-australia/).

## How an application runs

1. **Application.** Your prescriber sends EnableNSW the application.
2. **Review.** EnableNSW checks it against the program guidelines.
3. **Decision.** You and your prescriber are told in writing whether it is approved or declined.
4. **Purchase.** As funding becomes available, the device is bought and supplied.

## Electricity help

EnableNSW notes HRP clients may also qualify for the NSW Government's Life Support Rebate, claimed through the electricity retailer. See [life support electricity registration and rebates](/au/guides/life-support-electricity-australia/).

Other states run their own programs; see [home oxygen funding in Victoria and Queensland](/au/guides/home-oxygen-funding-victoria-queensland/).
