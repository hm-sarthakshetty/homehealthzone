## The ARTG

The Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) lists devices that may be supplied in Australia. The TGA says:

- A **sponsor** must apply to have each kind of medical device included on the ARTG.
- Each ARTG entry is unique to the **sponsor, manufacturer and kind of device**. A second importer of the same model needs its own entry.
- A condition of inclusion is that the device keeps meeting the TGA's Essential Principles for safety and performance.

Some Australian retailers print the ARTG number in the listing; Rapid Medical, for example, names ARTG 524233 for the Secure Plus 5 L concentrator. Otherwise, ask the seller, or search the ARTG on the TGA's site.

## Personal Importation Scheme

The scheme lets individuals import therapeutic goods **not** on the ARTG, usually by post or courier, if the conditions are met:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who uses it | You or an immediate family member: children, parents, grandparents, grandchildren, siblings, spouse or de facto partner |
| Quantity | A three-month supply in any one importation |
| TGA approval | Not required, if the other conditions are met |
| Resale | Not allowed: you cannot sell, supply or give the goods to others |
| Counterfeits | Prohibited in all circumstances |
| Health professionals | May import a device for personal use, but not for use in their practice |

### What it means for CPAP and oxygen users

- A machine bought overseas for your own use can come in under the scheme. Repeated or bulk orders are outside "three months' supply".
- A personally imported machine **cannot be resold** in Australia.
- The scheme is separate from **GST** and from **airline** rules. See [GST-free CPAP and oxygen](/au/guides/gst-free-cpap-oxygen-australia/) and [flying from Australia](/au/guides/flying-with-cpap-oxygen-australia/).

## Penalties

The TGA warns sponsors and health professionals who break the import and supply rules face civil or criminal penalties of up to **five years' imprisonment and/or 5,000 penalty units**, with one penalty unit valued at $330 under the Crimes Act 1914.
