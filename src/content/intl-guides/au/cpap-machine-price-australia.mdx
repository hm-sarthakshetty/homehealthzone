## Paying less

- **State programs.** NSW's [EnableNSW](/au/guides/enablensw-home-respiratory-program/) supplies CPAP for a $100 annual co-payment to pensioners and lower earners.
- **Private health extras.** ResMed Australia says extras cover may rebate part of the cost; check with your fund. See [Medicare, sleep studies and CPAP](/au/guides/medicare-sleep-study-cpap-australia/).
- **Trial first.** ResMed's shop lists an AirSense 11 AutoSet trial with up to two masks.
