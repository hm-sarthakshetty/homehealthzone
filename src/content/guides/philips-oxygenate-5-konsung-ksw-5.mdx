## Philips Respironics' FDA problems: what the record actually says

**The FDA announced a Philips Respironics consent decree on 9 April 2024, not a blanket withdrawal of every Philips FDA authorization.** The decree restricted specified manufacturing and distribution operations at or from named US facilities, subject to exceptions and compliance requirements. The action followed the recall of certain ventilators, CPAP and BiPAP machines involving sound-abatement foam. [FDA announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/federal-court-enters-consent-decree-against-philips-respironics-following-recall-certain-sleep).

The FDA record reviewed does not establish that the decree caused Philips to select Konsung or that Oxygenate 5 shares the recalled machines' defect. Separately, FDA 510(k) record **K223740** lists Konsung's KSW-5, KSN-5 and KSOC-5 model family with a substantially equivalent decision dated **5 October 2023**. That decision predates the decree but does not automatically establish authorization for the exact India-sold Oxygenate 5 configuration. [FDA K223740 record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K223740).
