## How to read the comparison

These are manufacturer-published specifications where a primary document is linked. They are not results from a shared bench test: noise measurement conditions, flow performance, and power reporting can differ between manufacturers. For that reason, a sound or wattage figure should be treated as a starting point for questions, not a universal ranking. Power is reported in watts only where the source reports watts; do not compare a VA figure with a W figure as if they were interchangeable.

An oxygen purity indicator or analyzer can alert the user to a concentration problem, but it does not replace prescribed-flow checks, maintenance, or service. Verify the indicator is included on the specific configuration, and ask the supplier how it signals a low reading and who services the unit.

## What to confirm before buying in India

Ask the supplier to put these details on the quotation or invoice:

- Exact model and variant, continuous-flow range, and oxygen concentration specification across the prescribed flow.
- Total payable price, including GST, delivery, installation, and any accessories charged separately.
- Package contents by item and quantity. For the HM-KV, the current manufacturer page confirms a nebulizer kit, fitted filter, and one extra HEPA filter; confirm all remaining contents and quantities with the seller.
- Warranty duration and operating-hour limit, whichever comes first, plus exclusions and the party responsible for service.
- Nearest authorized service contact, expected response time, and how replacement filters and other consumables are obtained.
- Compatibility with local mains supply and any stabilizer or backup power requirements; check the manufacturer’s voltage range rather than relying on a generic claim.
- For hill-station use, altitude limits for the precise model and flow setting.

The prescribed flow and a credible local service plan should decide the purchase.
