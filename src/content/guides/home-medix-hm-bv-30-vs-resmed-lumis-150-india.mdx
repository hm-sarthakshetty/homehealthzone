This is not a simple “same specification, lower price” comparison. HM-BV-30 uses **TVAPS** terminology; Lumis 150 uses **iVAPS**. The brochure names TVAPS as Target Volume Assured Pressure Support but does not describe its target-volume range or detailed pressure-adjustment rules. ResMed identifies iVAPS on applicable Lumis configurations. The mode names alone do not establish equivalence or interchangeability; the clinician must assess the exact documentation and prescription.

## Where HM-BV-30 wins

**Published price:** HM-BV-30 is listed at ₹38,000 before GST (₹39,900 including 5% GST); compare its full quote with the exact Lumis configuration. Its brochure lists pressure up to 30 cmH₂O. [HM-BV-30 listing](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-bv-30) · [HM-BV-30 brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-bv-30.pdf)

**Offline clinical detail:** Claro provides summaries, trends, waveforms, events, pressure, leak, airflow, and AHI views from the memory card on Windows or macOS. That supports periodic review without locking the patient to a cloud platform.

**Warranty and service:** the approved Home Medix PAP reference gives two years for the device including humidifier; adapter and SD card have one year each, and mask, tube and water tank have 90 days each under normal operation. It excludes responsibility for damage associated with operation outside specifications, accessories not provided or recommended by Home Medix, and technical adjustments without company consent. Unauthorized dismantling or repair can remove free-maintenance eligibility. Confirm start date, purchase-channel/registration rules, fault coverage, labour and transport terms. [Warranty and service terms](https://www.homemedix.in/resources/pap-warranty-and-service)

HM-BV-30 may be considered for a cost-limited home NIV plan only after the treating team reviews the available device documentation and confirms the prescribed settings and monitoring plan.

## Where Lumis 150 wins

**iVAPS ecosystem:** Lumis 150 is the correct choice when the clinician has prescribed or standardised on ResMed iVAPS rather than a generic volume target.

**Clinical familiarity and data:** ResMed’s ventilation platform, software, accessories, and service documentation are familiar to many sleep and respiratory teams. Connected configurations can simplify remote follow-up.

**Premium support footprint:** for complex neuromuscular, hypoventilation, or overlap cases, a mature device-specific clinical workflow can justify the higher price.

## What must match before switching

Do not map these machines using “target volume” alone. The clinician should verify:

- IPAP/EPAP range and maximum pressure support.
- Backup-rate behaviour and timed inspiration controls.
- How target ventilation or volume is calculated and adjusted.
- Trigger, cycle, rise-time, leak compensation, and alarm behaviour.
- Mask type, humidification, supplemental-oxygen connection, and report availability.

Read [TVAPS, AVAPS, and iVAPS devices in India](/guides/tvaps-avaps-ivaps-devices-india/) before treating the terms as equivalents.

## Verdict

HM-BV-30 may suit when its prescribed mode, documented capabilities, ₹39,900 tax-inclusive published price and local service fit the clinician’s plan. Lumis 150 is the stronger clinical-platform choice when iVAPS, ResMed’s ecosystem, or connected specialist follow-up is load-bearing. For complex ventilation, the cheapest compatible-looking machine is not necessarily compatible.
