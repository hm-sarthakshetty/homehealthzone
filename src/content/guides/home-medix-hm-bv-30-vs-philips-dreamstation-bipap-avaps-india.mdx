## What each device documents

Home Medix lists eight modes for HM-BV-30: CPAP, Auto CPAP, S, Auto S, T, ST, TVAPS and PC, across 4–30 cmH₂O; its published sound level is ≤30 dB(A), and its listed weight is 1.45 kg. The brochure names TVAPS as Target Volume Assured Pressure Support and describes the Sophisticated Adaptive Feedback (SAF) algorithm as analyzing inhalation/exhalation and working with trigger/cycle controls for breathing synchronisation. It does not state TVAPS target-volume ranges or detailed pressure-adjustment rules, so none are inferred here. The brochure also lists integrated humidification with levels 0–5, plus SD-card/Claro reporting for Windows and macOS. These are published specifications, not HHZ performance testing. [HM-BV-30 manufacturer brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-bv-30.pdf) · [Home Medix listing](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-bv-30)

Philips lists DreamStation BiPAP AVAPS modes as CPAP, S, S/T, T and PC, with an AVAPS target-volume feature and Digital Auto-Trak for breath triggering, cycling and leak compensation. Its product page lists an AVAPS target volume of 200–1,500 mL per breath, ventilation pressure of 4–30 cmH₂O and EPAP of 4–25 cmH₂O. These are Philips-published settings ranges, not a suggested prescription. The same page describes fixed/adaptive heated humidification and provides device-specific settings and limits. These describe Philips’ documented implementation; the feature names do not show that the therapy algorithms behave identically to TVAPS. [Philips India DreamStation BiPAP AVAPS details](https://www.philips.co.in/healthcare/product/HCINX1130T19/dreamstation-bipap-avaps-noninvasive-ventilator)

## Shortlist

**HM-BV-30 may suit a buyer when** the prescribed mode and device capabilities have been reviewed by the treating clinician, the documented pack and ₹39,900 total are suitable, and local Home Medix support and card-based reporting meet the care plan. Its broad mode list does not by itself establish suitability for a particular diagnosis or replacement prescription.

**DreamStation BiPAP AVAPS may suit a buyer when** the clinician specifically selects that device’s documented AVAPS feature, settings or data workflow, or continuity with an existing Philips configuration matters. Confirm local supply, warranty and support for the exact unit; a verified current price was not available for this comparison.

Do not infer clinical interchangeability from the labels TVAPS and AVAPS. A clinician should match the documented mode, interface, prescribed settings and monitoring plan to the patient. Do not change therapy or settings based on this guide.

## Before you order or switch

- Ask the prescriber which device and mode are prescribed and why.
- Have the clinician confirm the exact model and settings for the intended use.
- Request the complete GST-inclusive quote and written accessory list.
- Confirm humidifier, mask/interface, circuit, power supply and consumables.
- Confirm warranty issuer, exclusions, claim steps and location-specific support.
- Agree on the report format, review schedule and follow-up plan with the care team.
