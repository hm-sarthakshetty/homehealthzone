## Price and what is in the box

Home Medix publishes HM-CV-20 at **₹30,000 before 5% GST, or ₹31,500 including GST**, checked on 28 October 2026. For this update, Home Medix confirmed a pack of one HM-CV-20 device with integrated humidifier, one nasal mask, one reusable filter, one power cord, one SD card, one breathing circuit and one carrying bag; the current model page confirms this supplied pack. Check the items against the invoice before payment. [HM-CV-20 India listing](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-cv-20)

We could not verify a current written India quote for the matching BMC GII Auto CPAP configuration. Ask the seller to state the full model code, humidifier, mask, circuit, filters, card, warranty issuer and final GST-inclusive total. Avoid comparing an older or incomplete online price with the current HM-CV-20 total.

## Modes, pressure and humidification

The HM-CV-20 brochure lists CPAP and Auto CPAP modes, 4–20 cmH₂O pressure, integrated humidification with levels 0–5, ramp settings, sound ≤30 dB(A) and a weight of 1.45 kg. It also lists automatic altitude compensation and leak compensation. These are manufacturer-published specifications, not HHZ bench measurements. [HM-CV-20 manufacturer brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-cv-20.pdf) · [Home Medix product listing](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-cv-20)

BMC’s GII family page lists the E-20A-H-O as the 4–20 cmH₂O CPAP/Auto CPAP model and describes its Eco Smart humidification, Reslex exhalation relief, sleep-event detection and automatic leakage and altitude compensation. Because “GII” covers multiple models and screen/configuration variants, ask the seller to identify the exact E-20A model on the quote. [BMC GII model and feature page](https://en.bmc-medical.com/col194/59)

## Data and follow-up

Home Medix states that its Claro software for Windows and macOS reads therapy data from the device card and presents event labels and therapy summaries. The brochure identifies obstructive apnoea, central apnoea, hypopnoea, flow limitation and snoring as event categories; the report can support clinical review but does not diagnose a condition by itself. [Home Medix HM-CV-20 brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-cv-20.pdf)

BMC lists SD card, iCode/iCode QR, a quick report on the device screen, PAP Link App, PAP Link PC and PAP Link Web in its GII family information. Which channels are usable depends on the exact model and connectivity accessories. Before buying, ask the prescriber or clinic which report format it can review and verify that the exact device will export it. [BMC GII reporting options](https://en.bmc-medical.com/col194/59)

## Warranty and service

The Home Medix approved PAP warranty reference states two years for the device including humidifier, one year each for power adapter and SD card, and 90 days each for mask, tube and water tank under normal operation. It excludes responsibility for damage associated with operation outside the device specifications, use of accessories not provided or recommended by Home Medix, and technical adjustments made without company consent. Unauthorized dismantling or repair can remove eligibility for free maintenance. Warranty start date, purchase-channel or registration rules, fault coverage, labour and transport charges must be confirmed for the specific purchase. [Home Medix PAP warranty and service terms](https://www.homemedix.in/resources/pap-warranty-and-service)

For BMC, get the warranty duration, provider, service route and claim conditions in writing for the actual India-sold machine. Do not assume global manufacturer terms apply to an imported or reseller-supplied unit. For either brand, ask how to get the machine assessed in your city and what the quote includes.

## Shortlist

**Choose HM-CV-20 when** its CPAP/APAP range matches the prescription, you want the published complete package and current fixed total, and the documented Home Medix warranty and service route work for your location. Its specified mode range is straightforward; it is not a bilevel ventilator.

**Choose BMC GII Auto CPAP when** the seller confirms the E-20A-H-O variant, provides a complete GST-inclusive quote and package, and its local warranty, reporting workflow or clinician familiarity is a better fit. GII is a family name, so the precise model matters.

No comparison here establishes therapeutic equivalence for an individual. Confirm diagnosis, prescribed mode and settings with the treating clinician; do not change therapy based on this guide.

## Before you order

- Ask for the full model and suffix on the pro forma invoice.
- Compare final GST-inclusive totals and list every included component.
- Confirm the mask size/type, compatible tube and humidifier chamber supplied.
- Ask the seller to state warranty coverage, claim route and service contact for your city.
- Confirm that your clinic can review the available report/card workflow.
- Match mode and settings to the prescription with the clinician.
