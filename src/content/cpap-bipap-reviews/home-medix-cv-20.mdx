**Evidence basis:** This review summarizes the supplied HM-CV-20 brochure and dated Home Medix catalogue information confirmed for 28 October 2026. HHZ has not independently tested the device. Product specifications and software descriptions are manufacturer-published claims.

## Published specifications

The brochure lists **CPAP and Auto CPAP** modes, a **4–20 cmH₂O** pressure range, **≤30 dBA** sound level, and a weight of **1.45 kg**. It describes an integrated humidifier with levels 0–5, ramp settings from 0 to 60 minutes, EPFlex comfort settings from 0 to 3, leak compensation, and automatic altitude compensation. It also lists leak, patient-disconnect and power-failure alarms.

The brochure says the device stores up to **365 nights** of data. It describes Home Medix Claro software for reviewing therapy information from the device’s SD card on Windows or macOS, and a mobile app using a device QR code. These descriptions do not establish a cloud service or automatic remote monitoring; HHZ has not independently verified the software workflow.

## Price, supplied pack and warranty

The dated catalogue price confirmed for 28 October 2026 is **₹30,000 before 5% GST, or ₹31,500 including GST**. Confirm the current total and availability before ordering.

The pack confirmed by Home Medix for this update contains **one HM-CV-20 device with integrated humidifier, one nasal mask, one reusable filter, one power cord, one SD card, one breathing circuit and one carrying bag**. The public listing may not enumerate every confirmed item; match the written order to this list. [HM-CV-20 brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-cv-20.pdf) · [Home Medix product page](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-cv-20).

Home Medix’s published terms state **2 years for the device, including humidifier, under normal operation**. Accessory terms are **1 year each for the power adapter and SD card; 90 days each for the mask, tube and water tank**. The terms exclude coverage for damage caused by operation outside device specifications, use of accessories not provided or recommended by Home Medix, or technical adjustments without company consent. Unauthorized disassembly or repair can remove eligibility for free maintenance. Warranty start date, registration and purchase-channel rules, labour, claim and transport terms require confirmation. [Home Medix PAP warranty and service terms](https://www.homemedix.in/resources/pap-warranty-and-service).

## Review scope

This review reports published features and does not establish treatment outcomes, equivalence to another manufacturer’s algorithm, or suitability for an individual. A feature such as event detection is not treatment for those events. This review does not assess clinical suitability or individualized treatment.

Confirm the included accessories, written device and accessory warranty, and the local service route with the seller.
