**Evidence basis:** This review summarizes the supplied HM-BV-30 brochure and dated Home Medix catalogue information confirmed for 28 October 2026. HHZ has not independently tested the device. Product specifications and software descriptions are manufacturer-published claims.

## Published specifications

The brochure describes a BPAP device with TVAPS and lists **eight modes**: CPAP, Auto CPAP, S, Auto S, T, ST, TVAPS and PC. Its listed pressure range is **4–30 cmH₂O**, sound level **≤30 dBA**, pressure accuracy **±0.5 cmH₂O**, weight **1.45 kg**, and dimensions **253 × 168 × 121 mm**. It lists integrated humidification with levels 0–5, EPFlex settings in CPAP modes, ramp settings, automatic altitude compensation, leak compensation, and leak, patient-disconnect and power-failure alarms.

Home Medix describes Claro software for reviewing therapy information from the device’s SD card on Windows or macOS, and mobile QR-code resources. The brochure describes therapy review and reporting; HHZ has not independently verified the software workflow or any network behavior.

TVAPS and other mode names are manufacturer terminology. Their inclusion does not establish equivalence to another manufacturer’s implementation, expected clinical outcomes, or suitability for a specific prescription.

## Price, supplied pack and warranty

The dated catalogue price confirmed for 28 October 2026 is **₹38,000 before 5% GST, or ₹39,900 including GST**. Confirm the current total and availability before ordering.

The pack confirmed by Home Medix for this update contains **one HM-BV-30 device with integrated humidifier, one full-face mask, one reusable filter, one power cord, one SD card, one breathing circuit and one carrying bag**. The public listing may not enumerate every confirmed item; match the written order to this list. [HM-BV-30 brochure](/resources/manufacturer-brochures/home-medix-hm-bv-30.pdf) · [Home Medix product page](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-bv-30).

Home Medix’s published terms state **2 years for the device, including humidifier, under normal operation**. Accessory terms are **1 year each for the power adapter and SD card; 90 days each for the mask, tube and water tank**. The terms exclude coverage for damage caused by operation outside device specifications, use of accessories not provided or recommended by Home Medix, or technical adjustments without company consent. Unauthorized disassembly or repair can remove eligibility for free maintenance. Warranty start date, registration and purchase-channel rules, labour, claim and transport terms require confirmation. [Home Medix PAP warranty and service terms](https://www.homemedix.in/resources/pap-warranty-and-service).

## Review scope

This review reports manufacturer-published specifications and does not establish treatment outcomes or clinical suitability. It does not assess individualized treatment. Confirm the supplied accessories, written warranty terms and local support with the seller before purchase.
