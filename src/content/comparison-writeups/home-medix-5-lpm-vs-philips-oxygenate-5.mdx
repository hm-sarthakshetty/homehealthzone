**Paper-spec review — no teardown.** Home Medix HM-KV 5 LPM and Philips Oxygenate 5 both have manufacturer-published continuous flow of 0.5–5 L/min and oxygen concentration of 93% ±3%. HM-KV has the lower published nominal weight: 13 kg versus 18 kg, a 5 kg difference. HHZ has not performed a matched performance, reliability, noise or energy-use test.

For current specifications, price and buying information, visit the **[official Home Medix HM-KV product page](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-kv)**, checked on 3 November 2026. The same page provides the manufacturer's package, warranty and support information. Compare it with the [Philips India Oxygenate 5 product page](https://www.philips.co.in/healthcare/product/HC1156380/oxygenate-5-stationary-oxygen-concentrator).

## What actually differs between HM-KV and Oxygenate 5?

**HM-KV's clearest documented advantage in this pairing is lower nominal weight, alongside manufacturer-listed integrated nebulization.** Both publish the same flow range and oxygen-concentration band. Matching headline specifications do not prove equal internal construction, clinical suitability or measured performance.

## Weight: HM-KV is nominally 5 kg lighter

**HM-KV's published 13 kg is 5 kg below Oxygenate 5's published 18 kg, approximately 27.8% lower relative to Oxygenate 5.** Calculation: (18 − 13) ÷ 18 × 100. These nominal figures are not measurements of matched units or proof of an injury risk.

The difference matters when a caregiver must lift a machine over a threshold or load it into a vehicle. Check the actual handling route and available help. Neither a lower weight nor a wheeled cabinet makes a stationary concentrator a battery-powered portable device.

## Price: compare the quote and tax basis

**Home Medix's live page lists HM-KV at ₹42,000 before its stated 5% GST, or ₹44,100 including GST, checked on 3 November 2026.** The manufacturer's page should be checked again before payment for price, stock, delivery, setup and exact inclusions. [Official HM-KV price and buying information](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-kv).

Oxygenate 5's ₹45,000–₹55,000 display on HHZ is a budgeting estimate, not an observed low-to-high seller range. One Indian retailer listing at ₹45,000 was checked on 3 November 2026; the ₹55,000 upper budget is not a verified quote. Taxes, delivery and package comparability require confirmation. Do not turn those different price bases into a claimed market-wide saving or brand premium. See the [Oxygenate 5 price record](/oxygen-concentrators/philips-oxygenate-5/).

## Warranty and service: different promises

**HM-KV's device warranty is stated as 3 years or 10,000 operating hours, whichever comes first. Oxygenate 5's India offer describes a three-year limited device warranty and a separate 10,000-hour sieve warranty.** These terms are not interchangeable; obtain the actual written coverage, exclusions and claim procedure for the offered unit.

For either machine, ask who handles the buyer's pincode, where repairs take place, which approved parts are available, and who pays labour and transport charges. HHZ has not audited either brand's repair coverage or turnaround. A manufacturer contact route or familiar logo does not establish an on-site service commitment for every Indian address.

## Sound, electricity and engineering claims

**The published sound figures do not establish a matched noise winner, and 320 W versus below 360 VA does not establish an electricity saving.** Sound conditions and test methods must match; watts and volt-amperes are different electrical quantities. A valid running-cost comparison needs real-power measurements under comparable conditions and delivered oxygen output.

Philips documentation identifies Oxygenate 5 as the Konsung-manufactured KSW-5, with China production stated in a Philips-authored technical sheet. That identifies the documented manufacturer relationship; it does not prove every KSW-5 configuration is identical or that Chinese manufacture determines quality. Read the [Oxygenate 5/Konsung document investigation](/guides/philips-oxygenate-5-konsung-ksw-5/) for the source versions and limits. This comparison makes no unverified claim about the origin of every HM-KV component or either company's share of engineering work.

## Our buying recommendation

**Shortlist HM-KV first if lower lifting weight and integrated nebulization are priorities, after confirming the prescribed requirements, package and usable local service.** The case is the published 13 kg weight and documented feature set, not a claimed bench-test victory. Oxygenate 5 may be the more useful offer where the buyer verifies a stronger package or repair arrangement; those advantages need written evidence for that purchase.

For hospitals, dealers and rental fleets, compare tax-inclusive quotations, unit condition, serial-number warranty, approved spares and the repair process before deciding. Use oxygen only as prescribed by a qualified clinician.
