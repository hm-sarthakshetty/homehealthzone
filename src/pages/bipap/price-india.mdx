# BiPAP Machine Price in India (2026)

## Quick price answer

A **BiPAP machine in India generally costs ₹23,000–₹40,000 for entry and mid-range auto-bilevel or ST models, ₹40,000–₹70,000 for stronger ST and volume-assured machines, and ₹70,000–₹1.4 lakh for premium imported systems**. The blower price is not the complete cost: allow for a compatible mask, humidifier, heated tube where required, filters, data access, setup and follow-up.

| BiPAP category | Practical machine-price band | Typical use case |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Auto BiPAP / bilevel | ₹23,000–₹40,000 | Pressure support where CPAP is not tolerated or bilevel is prescribed |
| BiPAP ST | ₹35,000–₹70,000 | Spontaneous/timed backup rate when prescribed |
| VAPS / AVAPS / iVAPS | ₹38,000–₹85,000 | Volume-assured ventilation when prescribed |
| Premium imported ST / VAPS | ₹65,000–₹1.4 lakh | Advanced modes, established data ecosystem or specific clinical requirement |

Do not select a BiPAP mode from price alone. Auto bilevel, ST and volume-assured ventilation are not interchangeable; the prescription and titration settings should determine the category before the brand shortlist.

## What changes the final BiPAP cost

### Therapy mode

ST adds a timed backup rate. VAPS, AVAPS, iVAPS and TVAPS add a volume-targeting layer. Those modes cost more because the machine, setup and follow-up are more complex. Paying for them is justified only when the prescription calls for them.

### Included humidifier and mask

A listing that looks ₹5,000 cheaper may omit the heated humidifier or include no mask. Ask for a line-item quotation showing the blower, humidifier, power supply, tube, carry bag, SD card or modem, mask and warranty.

### Service and data support

The useful warranty is the one a local provider can execute. Confirm who will read therapy data, correct settings under clinician direction, diagnose alarms and provide a loaner if a ventilation-dependent user cannot wait for repair.

### Imported stock and warranty status

For imported ResMed or Philips models, verify serial number, manufacturing/import date, Indian warranty eligibility and the exact service route. A low grey-market price can become expensive if the machine has no local warranty or compatible spares.

| Cost item | Typical planning range |

| BiPAP machine | ₹23,000–₹1.4 lakh |
| Full-face or nasal mask | ₹3,000–₹15,000 |
| Heated tube, if separate | ₹2,500–₹5,500 |
| Replacement cushions and filters | ₹1,500–₹6,000 per year |
| Setup, titration or follow-up | Varies by provider and clinical pathway |
| Backup power, if clinically needed | Varies by required runtime and load |

The cheapest safe purchase is therefore not always the machine with the lowest sticker price. Compare the **complete first-year cost** and the provider's ability to support the prescribed mode.

## BiPAP rental versus buying

Rental can make sense for a supervised short-term trial, post-discharge bridge or while the prescription is being finalised. For long-term therapy, repeated monthly rent often overtakes purchase, and the user may still receive an older fleet unit.

Before renting, record the machine model, mode capability, blower hours, humidifier condition, mask status, deposit, monthly charge, service response time and replacement-unit policy. Never accept an ST or VAPS prescription on a machine that only provides ordinary auto bilevel.

## Buying checklist

1. Get the exact mode and settings from the treating team.
2. Compare only machines that support that prescribed mode.
3. Request a complete written quote, including humidifier and mask.
4. Verify serial number, stock age and Indian warranty before payment.
5. Confirm local setup, data review, spare parts and loaner support.
6. Check mask fit while lying down at therapy pressure.
7. Plan backup power and an escalation route if therapy interruption is unsafe.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the lowest BiPAP machine price in India?

Entry listings begin around ₹23,000–₹28,000, but the lowest price should not decide the purchase. Confirm that the machine supports the prescribed mode, includes the required humidifier and has a real Indian service path.

### Why is BiPAP more expensive than CPAP?

BiPAP independently controls inspiratory and expiratory pressure. ST and volume-assured systems add backup-rate or ventilation-targeting functions, which increase hardware, software and setup complexity. See the separate [CPAP price guide](/cpap/price-india/) for a clean comparison.

### Which BiPAP is best under ₹40,000?

The answer depends on required mode. Oxymed AirSmart Auto is a price candidate for auto bilevel; Oxymed ST with VAPS, Home Medix HM-BV-30, BMC G3 B30VT and Deckmount VT 200 are value candidates when a compatible volume-assured mode is prescribed. Compare them using the [BiPAP ranking](/top-5/bipap-machines/) and local service proof.

### Can I buy a BiPAP without a sleep study or prescription?

Do not self-select bilevel mode or pressures. A sleep or ventilation assessment and clinician-directed setup are important because the wrong mode or settings can leave events untreated or create a different ventilation problem.

## Bottom line

Budget **₹23,000–₹40,000 for value bilevel, ₹40,000–₹70,000 for stronger ST/VAPS options, and ₹70,000+ for premium imported systems**. Decide the required therapy mode first, then compare complete package cost, data access, Indian warranty and local service—not the headline discount.
