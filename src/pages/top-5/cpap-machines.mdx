A CPAP machine delivers pressurised air through a mask to help keep the airway open during sleep. An auto-adjusting CPAP (APAP) varies pressure within a configured range; a bilevel device uses different pressures for inhalation and exhalation. These categories are not interchangeable purchase decisions: the prescribed therapy comes first. [NHLBI explains CPAP and other positive airway pressure devices](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/cpap).

## How to choose a CPAP machine

**Therapy and follow-up:** Check the prescribed mode and pressure range, then ask how the clinic expects to obtain therapy data. Connectivity and supplied accessories can vary by Indian SKU; confirm them on the quote.

**Home or travel:** Compare packed weight, power options, humidification and mask compatibility. A smaller machine can make travel easier, but whether it meets the user's nightly needs should be checked before purchase.

**Mask and running cost:** A nasal mask covers the nose; nasal pillows sit at the nostrils, while a full-face mask covers both nose and mouth. A comfortable seal matters more than choosing by name alone. Check replacement cushions, filters and tubing as well as the initial price. See the [CPAP comfort guide](/guides/cpap-comfort-features-epr-flex-epflex/) and [APAP explainer](/cpap/apap/).

**Service in India:** Ask for written warranty terms, local repair arrangements and replacement-parts availability. The [Home Medix dealer and service network](/in/respiratory-care-experts/) identifies partners by region and describes services including setup, mask fitting, rentals, repairs and staff training. Service availability varies by partner.

## CPAP vs BiPAP

Fixed CPAP supplies one set pressure, while BiPAP is a bilevel form of positive airway pressure that uses higher inspiratory and lower expiratory pressures. APAP adjusts pressure automatically within its configured range; it is not the same category as bilevel ventilation. The [CPAP vs BiPAP guide](/guides/cpap-vs-bipap-indications/) explains the distinction. Choose the prescribed mode first, then compare the mask, support and total cost among compatible machines.

A bedside machine is usually judged on nightly practicalities: a readable display, humidifier care, accessible filters, noise, and the way the clinic receives data. The AirSense 10, AirSense 11 and HM-CV-20 catalogue records list integrated humidification. Compare the exact package: a heated tube, mask, and replacement filters may not all be included in every seller's quote.

## Humidification, masks and comfort

Heated humidification uses a water chamber; waterless systems use a replaceable heat-and-moisture exchange component. The second approach avoids packing a water chamber, but creates another compatibility and replacement-cost question. Follow the device instructions for cleaning and replacement; do not assume components are interchangeable across models.

A nasal mask rests over the nose, a nasal-pillow interface rests at the nostrils, and a full-face interface covers the nose and mouth. Sizing, headgear, facial hair and sleeping position can all affect the seal. Ask to review the fit with the supplier and check what happens if the supplied size is uncomfortable. The [NHLBI CPAP guide](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/cpap) describes the machine, hose and mask as parts of one system.

Ramp and expiratory relief are comfort features, but their names and controls vary. Ask the clinician or supplier to explain which adjustments are available to the user and how to report persistent discomfort or leakage. A device's maximum advertised pressure does not tell a buyer what setting to use.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is APAP the same as BiPAP?

No. APAP automatically adjusts a positive airway pressure within a configured range. BiPAP is a bilevel form of positive airway pressure, with separate inspiratory and expiratory pressures. See the [CPAP vs BiPAP guide](/guides/cpap-vs-bipap-indications/) for a fuller explanation.

### Does the quietest published number guarantee the quietest experience?

No. Published sound levels help identify what to investigate, but the measurement conditions and the complete setup may differ. Mask vent noise, placement and tubing can affect what a sleeper hears. Ask for measurement conditions and, where possible, see the configuration in use.

### Can I use any nasal mask with a travel CPAP?

Not necessarily. Mask, connector and tubing compatibility depend on the machine. AirMini requires a compatible system, while the Z2 catalogue record lists a universal tube adapter. Check the specific mask and any adapter before buying either machine.

### Is a humidifier included in every CPAP quote?

No. A catalogue may describe a feature while a seller's bundle has different contents. Ask for an itemised quote identifying the chamber, tubing, mask, power supply and any optional accessories.

### What is the difference between a nasal mask and nasal pillows?

A nasal mask covers the nose; nasal pillows seal at the nostrils. Both connect to a hose, but fit and compatible sizes differ. Compare the complete interface and headgear, not only the cushion shape.

### Should I choose the cheapest machine?

First check the prescribed mode and the mask, data and support requirements. Then compare the complete package and recurring supplies. A lower machine price can be offset by accessories or a service arrangement that does not meet the user's needs.
