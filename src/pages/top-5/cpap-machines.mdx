A CPAP machine supplies pressurised air through a mask to help keep the airway open during sleep. An auto-adjusting CPAP (APAP) changes pressure within a configured range. The prescribing clinician's mode and settings come first; a shortlist cannot determine which therapy is appropriate. [NHLBI's overview of CPAP and other positive airway pressure devices](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/cpap) explains the treatment categories.

## Choosing a machine and checking the quote

**Prescription and follow-up:** Match the prescribed mode and pressure range. Ask the clinic how it will review use, leak, pressure and event data, and whether the exact device can provide that information in a format it can access. An app or SD card does not by itself establish clinician monitoring.

**Home or travel:** Compare the packed system, not only the device. Include mask, tubing, power supply and any proprietary connector or waterless humidification consumables. Breas currently identifies the Z2 range as discontinued, which makes stock age, warranty and future service important quote questions.

**Mask and recurring cost:** Ask for the mask model and size, compatibility confirmation, replacement cushion, filter and tube prices, and the process if the initial fit is poor. The Home Medix nasal mask and accessory pack listed above were confirmed for this update; the current product page confirms this pack.

**Warranty and service in India:** Get the warranty issuer, serial-number region, start date, coverage exclusions, claim contact, assessment location, labour/transport charges and repair timing in writing. A manufacturer's global support page does not prove a local dealer's service coverage. Home Medix's published PAP terms specify a two-year device/humidifier period and shorter accessory periods under normal operation. The reference excludes damage associated with operation outside device specifications, unsuitable accessories and unapproved technical changes; unauthorised disassembly can affect eligibility for free maintenance. Claim process, registration, labour and transport terms remain to be confirmed. [Full terms](https://www.homemedix.in/resources/pap-warranty-and-service).

## CPAP and BiPAP are different prescriptions

Fixed CPAP uses one prescribed pressure; APAP varies pressure within its configured range. Bilevel PAP uses separate inspiratory and expiratory pressures and may have additional timed or volume-assured modes. These devices are not interchangeable upgrades. See the [CPAP vs BiPAP guide](/guides/cpap-vs-bipap-indications/) and [APAP explainer](/cpap/apap/), and discuss any change with the prescribing clinician.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the current Home Medix HM-CV-20 price?

The manufacturer page lists ₹30,000 before 5% GST, or ₹31,500 including GST, checked 28 October 2026. The manufacturer pack confirmed for this update contains one machine, nasal mask, reusable filter, power cord, SD card, breathing circuit and carrying bag. ### Does the warranty include the mask and humidifier?

The published Home Medix terms give the device including humidifier two years under normal operation. Adapter and SD card are one year each; mask, tube and water tank are 90 days each. The warranty reference excludes damage from operation outside specifications, unsuitable accessories or unapproved technical changes; unauthorised disassembly can affect eligibility for free maintenance. Confirm claim/registration, labour and transport arrangements before purchase.

### Is Breas Z2 Auto still being made?

Breas currently says its Z Sleep Travel CPAP line, which includes Z2 Auto, has been discontinued. It says service parts and patient accessories are available through October 2030. Ask an India seller to verify the unit's age, condition, warranty and service path.

### Can I use the same mask on every CPAP?

Not always. Connector, tube and mask requirements vary, especially on travel systems. Have the seller confirm compatibility for the exact device and mask size before ordering.
