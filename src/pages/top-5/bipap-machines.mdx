Bilevel positive airway pressure (BiPAP/BPAP) supplies different inspiratory and expiratory pressures. Some devices also offer a timed backup breath or a volume-assured mode. The right device depends on the clinician's prescription and setup; a product shortlist cannot establish that BiPAP is indicated or choose settings. See the [CPAP vs BiPAP guide](/guides/cpap-vs-bipap-indications/) and [BiPAP-ST guide](/guides/bipap-st-devices-india/).

## Compare prescription, package and support

**Mode before brand:** Ask the prescriber to identify the required mode, pressure range, backup-rate requirement and, if applicable, the volume target and monitoring plan. Auto-bilevel (such as VAuto) and timed or volume-assured ventilation are different capabilities. Do not select a mode or change settings from a product page.

**Full system cost:** For every quote, confirm exact device/SKU, GST, mask model and size, humidifier and chamber, tube, power supply, filter, SD card, bag, setup/training, delivery and expected replacement supplies. A product-family price or brochure does not guarantee those items are in a particular seller's box.

**Warranty and service:** Request the warranty issuer, serial-number region, start date, exclusions, registration or purchase-channel requirements, claim contact, assessment location, transport/labour charges and repair turnaround in writing. A manufacturer's global product or support page does not prove India-side dealer service. Home Medix's published PAP terms list a two-year device/humidifier period and shorter accessory periods under normal operation; some claim and service details remain unconfirmed.

**Data workflow:** Ask whether the device saves data to an SD card or sends it through a connected service, what the clinician can see, and who will retrieve or review the report. Cloud branding alone does not establish that the particular machine is connected or that the clinic receives its data.

### Is an auto-BiPAP the same as ST or TVAPS?

No. Auto-bilevel adjusts pressures within its configured limits. ST adds a timed backup breath; TVAPS is a volume-assured pressure-support mode. The exact combination and operation differ by device. Ask the prescribing clinician which mode is intended.
