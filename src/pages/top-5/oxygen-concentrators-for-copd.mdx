## How we ranked

Long-term COPD home use weighs differently from a generic 5 LPM ranking. For chronic-condition buyers we re-weight the rubric toward published warranty terms, dealer and service-centre footprint, power draw at typical operating flow, and bedside noise. Continuous-flow capability (not pulse-dose) is required — pulse-dose units are inappropriate for stable resting hypoxaemia. Flow-rate floor matters too: some COPD prescriptions run as low as 0.5–1 LPM overnight, so units that cannot titrate below 1 LPM are excluded from the top tier. All performance numbers cited below come from manufacturer brochures and e-commerce product listings; HHZ has not run bench tests on these specific units. The full rubric is at [our methodology page](/methodology/).

## The top 5

### 1. Oxymed Mini 5 LPM — 8.3

**Legacy price snapshot (not a current offer)** — ₹35,400 (listed MRP ₹59,900; capture date not confirmed). Medequip’s manufacturer product page lists 1–5 L/min, 13.9 kg, 45 dB, 390 W, and a 3-year warranty. Its specifications table says 90–96% concentration while page prose says 93% ±3%; confirm the specification for the exact unit. ([Medequip Oxymed Mini product page](https://medequip.co.in/products/oxygen-concentrator-mini-5-lpm))

**Pros**
- Broadest dealer and service-centre footprint of any concentrator brand in the Indian indigenous tier — the brand claims 40+ service centres and the public dealer-listing density is the highest in the category. For a chronic-use patient who will need service calls over a multi-year horizon, this is the single most important attribute.
- Active CDSCO manufacturing licence `MFG/MD/2024/000436`, most recently re-issued 3 February 2026 — signals ongoing manufacturer engagement with the platform, which matters for spare-parts continuity over a long ownership horizon.
- The manufacturer page lists 45 dB sound and 13.9 kg. Its flow range begins at 1 L/min, so it does not cover a prescribed setting below 1 L/min. The manufacturer page also carries the medical-device-use disclaimer in the update note above.
- The Indian manufacturing operation (with Chinese subassembly sourcing — see transparency note below) puts service escalation in-country; warranty claims don't depend on an overseas importer relationship.

**Cons**
- The Indian manufacturing operation operates a kit-assembly pattern: the same model codes (`AR-5-N`, `MAOXY 05`) appear on both Medequip's CDSCO manufacturing licence and their import licences for units sourced from Shenyang Aerti Tech Co., China. The Indian operation is final-stage assembly, not full indigenous build from raw components. Buyers prioritising indigenous supply chain should weigh this against the service-network advantage.
- 1 L/min flow floor; patients with a prescribed setting below 1 L/min should choose a model whose manufacturer supports it. ([CAIRE VisionAire 5 specifications](https://www.caireinc.com/product/airsep-visionaire/))
- 390 W power draw is mid-pack — annual electricity cost on 24/7 use sits between the most efficient (AirSep Visionaire 5 at 290 W) and the least efficient indigenous peers (550 VA+).

**Use status** — suitability for prescribed COPD oxygen therapy is unresolved until Medequip clarifies the product page’s not-a-medical-device/use disclaimer. Do not rely on the service-network claim alone to select a therapy device.

### 2. Home Medix HM-KV 5 LPM — 8.2

**Price and configuration snapshot** — manufacturer-published price ₹42,000 before 5% GST (₹44,100 including GST), 13 kg, 0.5–5 L/min continuous flow, 93% ±3% purity, ≤40 dB(A) published sound, 320 W draw per the product page, and 3 years or 10,000 operating hours of warranty, whichever comes first. The supplied brochure’s feature panel says 320 W while its specification grid says 320 VA; confirm the rating on the offered unit documentation. The current manufacturer page confirms the included nebulizer kit, fitted filter, and one extra HEPA filter; confirm all remaining package contents and quantities with the seller. ([Home Medix HM-KV product page](https://www.homemedix.in/shop/hm-kv))

**Pros**
- The 3-year or 10,000-operating-hour warranty term is published by the manufacturer; the hour limit may be reached before three calendar years in continuous use. Confirm coverage and exclusions on the seller’s written warranty.
- ≤40 dB(A) published sound and 13 kg weight make it a candidate for a bedside setup or room-to-room movement. Manufacturer figures are not directly comparable sound-bench results across brands.
- The manufacturer lists 320 W power consumption. Compare this figure with other manufacturer-reported watts, and confirm backup-power sizing against the actual unit and startup load.
- The 0.5 L/min lower limit may suit a prescription below 1 L/min. CAIRE lists the VisionAire 5 range as 1–5 L/min, so it does not share the HM-KV’s published lower flow limit. ([CAIRE VisionAire 5 specifications](https://www.caireinc.com/product/airsep-visionaire/))

**Cons**
- Home Medix publishes a Bengaluru service route. Confirm assessment location, transport, charges and turnaround for the buyer’s pincode; the published contact does not establish nationwide coverage.
- Model-specific regulatory documents and warranty exclusions should be checked for the supplied unit. A company certification or brochure logo does not establish every model’s authorization.

**Best for** — the COPD patient whose prescribed flow benefits from a 0.5 L/min minimum and who values the published warranty term and sound specification, after confirming local service availability and the final package with the seller.

### 3. AirSep Visionaire 5 — 8.0

**Legacy price snapshot (not a current offer)** — ₹54,999 (listed MRP ₹80,640; capture date not confirmed). CAIRE lists VisionAire 5 as 1–5 L/min, 90% +5.5%/−3%, 13.6 kg, 39 dB(A), and 290 W at all flow settings. Confirm the exact India-supplied variant, monitor option, altitude rating, price, package, and support with the importer. ([CAIRE VisionAire 5 specifications](https://www.caireinc.com/product/airsep-visionaire/))

**Pros**
- CAIRE lists 290 W at all flow settings. This is a manufacturer specification, not an HHZ electricity-cost measurement; actual expense depends on use, tariff, voltage, and the supplied variant.
- Manufacturer lists VisionAire 5 flow from 1 to 5 L/min, so it is not suitable when a prescribed setting is below 1 L/min. Confirm the exact variant and prescription match. ([CAIRE VisionAire 5 specifications](https://www.caireinc.com/product/airsep-visionaire/))
- Complete alarm suite (loss of power, system malfunction, no-flow, Oxygen Purity Indicator) — the fullest unattended-operation safety stack of any unit in this top 5.
- Confirm altitude suitability for the offered VisionAire 5 using the applicable manufacturer manual and local conditions; the specification page linked here does not establish the altitude claim in the legacy snapshot.
- Imported under CDSCO `IMP/MD/2025/000687` to Ujjwal Medical Devices India Pvt Ltd — current and active import licence.

**Cons**
- The ₹54,999 figure is an undated legacy snapshot and cannot be compared with the current HM-KV manufacturer price as a live price difference. Obtain current written offers for both models.
- CAIRE/AirSep India service footprint is thinner than Philips Respironics, Oxymed, or BPL in the post-COVID Indian channel structure — service-call lead times in tier-2 and tier-3 cities can stretch to 14–21 days.
- Foreign-brand import (Bucket D); service continuity depends on Ujjwal Medical Devices maintaining the importer relationship and renewing the CDSCO import licence on schedule.

**Best for** — patients prescribed at least 1 L/min whose local importer confirms the exact VisionAire 5 configuration, support, and suitability for their altitude. CAIRE’s product page lists 290 W; that figure alone does not establish the lowest energy cost for every use case.

### 4. Nidek Nuvo Lite 5 LPM — 7.8

**Nidek Mark 5 Nuvo Lite 925 (230 V/50 Hz)** — the manufacturer IFU lists 0.125–5 L/min, 87–95.5% at 5 L/min, 14.5 kg, and 300 W average. Sound is unresolved across documents: family IFU says &lt;58 dBA, a 925 brochure says 40 dBA typical, and an official OCSI guide gives 45 to &lt;40 dBA depending on model. Verify the offered model/revision and monitor. ([Nidek Mark 5 Nuvo Lite IFU](https://www.nidekmedical.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/2010-8401ce-rev-g-ifu-nuvo-lite-family.pdf); [925 brochure](https://www.somatco.com/NuvoLite-Mark5-NIDEK.pdf); [Nidek OCSI guide](https://www.nidekmedical.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2010-8401ce-rev-b-nuvo-lite-ocsi-english-single.pdf))

**Legacy price snapshot (not a current offer)** — ₹57,599 (listed MRP ₹66,240; capture date not confirmed). Confirm exact 925 offer, Indian warranty, package, and authorized support with the importer.

**Pros**
- A 925 brochure lists 40 dBA typical, but other Nuvo Lite family documents give different sound values. Those specifications do not establish a direct noise ranking against the HM-KV.
- The official 925 IFU lists 300 W average for the 230 V model; use the exact offered model and source when sizing power.
- Premium build quality and Nidek's long-standing reputation in respiratory equipment — patients and caregivers familiar with the brand have confidence in the platform.
- Imported under CDSCO `IMP/MD/2024/000202` to Nidek Medical India Pvt Ltd from the US manufacturing site (Nidek Medical Products Inc.), with adjacent licence `IMP/MD/2025/000484` covering same-class units sourced from Shenyang Aerti — buyers should confirm with the dealer which manufacturing origin applies to the specific unit being purchased.

**Cons**
- The legacy price snapshot is not a current quote; obtain a complete written offer for the exact 925 unit and service arrangement.
- Foreign-brand import (Bucket D); service continuity depends on Nidek Medical India's licence renewal cycle. The original Nuvo Lite import licence dates to March 2024.
- The Shenyang Aerti adjacent licence introduces ambiguity about manufacturing origin on units sold through some Indian channels — same-spec Aerti-built units may carry different long-term service profile than US-built Nidek units.

**Best for** — buyers whose prescription fits the documented 925 flow range and who can verify the exact unit, sound specification, monitor option, and local service. The checked Nuvo Lite documents do not establish one definitive sound value across model revisions.

### 5. DeVilbiss 5 LPM (Compact 525) — 7.5

**Drive DeVilbiss 525 series (525DS / 525KS)** — the official manual distinguishes the 115 V/60 Hz 525DS from the 220–230 V/50 Hz 525KS. Both list 0.5–5 L/min and 16.3 kg; concentration is 87–96% for 525DS and 93% ±3% for 525KS. For 525DS the manual lists 310 W average and 48 dBA; for 525KS at 230 V/50 Hz it lists 312 W average (296 W at ≤1.2 L/min) and 40 dBA under ISO 8359. Verify exact SKU and India warranty/support. ([Drive DeVilbiss 525 series product page and manual](https://shop.drivemedical.com/us/en/products/respiratory/oxygen-therapy/oxygen-concentrators/stationary-concentrators/devilbiss-5-liter-oxygen-concentrator/p/525DS?siteName=Drive+Medical+US+Site&siteUid=us))

**Legacy price snapshot (not a current offer)** — ₹45,984 (listed MRP ₹86,400; capture date not confirmed). Confirm exact unit, full package, Indian warranty, and authorized service on a current written quote.

**Pros**
- Power figures depend on the model: the manufacturer lists 310 W average for 525DS and 312 W average for 525KS at 230 V/50 Hz. Confirm the supplied SKU and compare against matching voltage/configuration data.
- Drive DeVilbiss has an established Indian service footprint with 22 authorised service centres claimed nationally — solid coverage for chronic-use service needs.
- Long-standing brand reputation in oxygen therapy; technicians across most Indian medical-equipment service networks are familiar with the platform.
- Imported under CDSCO `IMP/MD/2025/000547` to Sanrai Med India, which also handles the Inogen and DeVilbiss portable lines — a meaningful single-importer service relationship.

**Cons**
- Manufacturer lists 48 dBA for 525DS and 40 dBA for 525KS at 50 Hz. These values are configuration- and measurement-specific; verify the supplied SKU before weighing bedside sound.
- 16.3 kg chassis is the heaviest in this top 5 — less suited to bedroom-to-bedroom transitions, more of a fixed-installation unit.
- The ₹45,984 figure is an undated legacy snapshot, not a current comparison with the HM-KV manufacturer price. Confirm the exact 525-series SKU, total price, and importer service in writing.

**Best for** — buyers who can confirm the exact 525-series voltage/model variant and authorized Indian support. The manufacturer publishes different sound and power figures for 525DS and 525KS, so the nameplate matters before making bedside or power comparisons.
