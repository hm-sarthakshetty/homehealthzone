# Respiratory device manufacturers in India

    A manufacturer’s identity, a brand name and a local seller are different facts. Check the documents for the exact device before describing it as made in India.


## Manufacturer profile

    [Home Medix](https://homehealthzone.com/manufacturers/home-medix/) — a Bengaluru-based respiratory and home-care brand that describes itself as a manufacturer. The profile separates its published claims from model-specific evidence still to be checked.


## How to verify origin and responsibility


      - Check the device label and current manual for the legal manufacturer, manufacturing address, model and country of manufacture. A brand or Indian business address alone does not establish where a unit was made.

      - Ask for the applicable manufacturing or import licence and its model or device scope. Match the legal entity, site and product to the documentation; a general certification does not establish origin for every model.

      - Identify the importer, distributor and seller where applicable, and confirm who handles installation, warranty and service at your location.


    For product comparisons, see the [Indian-made oxygen concentrators guide](https://homehealthzone.com/top-5/indian-made-oxygen-concentrators/) and verify the current label and documents for the unit offered.

    Recommendation: request the model-specific label and licence before relying on a country-of-origin or manufacturer claim.

## Manufacturer, importer and local dealer

    The legal manufacturer is the entity identified for a specific device in its applicable documentation. A brand owner may market a device made or assembled by another entity, an importer brings a device into India, and a dealer sells or supports it locally. Those roles can overlap, but one role does not prove another.

    Recommendation: ask for the current label, legal manufacturer and site, country of manufacture, and applicable licence for the exact model and unit. Confirm warranty and local service separately.
